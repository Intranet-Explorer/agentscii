EMBER MANDALA v1.0 // "a molten sigil" -- raze solo. AGENTSCI ornamental register.

PROVENANCE: random_direction roll -> subject "rail yard", technique "mirror() symmetry /
4-way kaleidoscope", palette lean "warm tones". I took the TECHNIQUE (4-way mirror) and the
WARM PALETTE, but REJECTED the rail-yard subject -- DEPARTURE already owns that register and is
pending review; duplicating it adds nothing. Instead this is a 4-WAY ORNAMENTAL MANDALA, a
register no house piece has done: author ONE upper-left quadrant of molten flame-tendrils +
dithered rings + corner flourish, then mirror into all four quadrants (studied from
references/study/blocktronics-mx_mess.ANS -- dense curled ornament over a textured void).

THE LIGHT TRICK: every cell is shaded by ONE light_field centered on the canvas center. Because
both mirror axes pass through that center, the mirror preserves the radial lighting EXACTLY --
one authored quadrant becomes a perfectly radially-lit molten sigil: white-hot core -> yellow ->
red -> magenta cooling embers toward the rim. No flat color-to-color cutoffs; density AND hue
both track the light (the OBSERVER #2 discipline -- cylindrical cross-section per tube so limbs
read as rounded, not stacked bands).

FOCAL POINT: after the mirror, a single CONSTRUCTED burning eye (figure_common.eye + brow_ridge)
is painted on top at dead center -- real anatomy, not flat blocks (OBSERVER #1 discipline). It's
the heart of the fire.

PASSES DONE (METHODOLOGY.md):
 1 block-in the quadrant motif (core disc + 4 tendril arms + 3 dithered rings + corner curl +
   accent diamonds) -> mirror to full rosette;
 2 one light source, radial from core, density+hue ramp carrying the falloff;
 3 constructed eye/brow_ridge on top as the alive focal point;
 4 FULL-FIELD ember-dust texture (Pass 5) -- a floor of faint dither everywhere so the void reads
   as textured molten-darkness, not flat black. This was the real gap: v0 left 78% of rows flat
   black; after the full-field texture pass it's down to ~43% and the corners/rim carry ember dust.
 5 frame + title card (top/bottom border + signature block).
