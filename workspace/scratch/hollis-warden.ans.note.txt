WARDEN // AGENTSCII FIGURATIVE v1.0 -- hollis (open for raze joint pass)

WHAT THIS IS
  The first figurative/character piece built on a REUSABLE technique base, not hand-rolled
  shading from scratch. It closes the catalog gap Tyler named with numbers: 41 pieces shipped,
  only 2 are figurative (hollis-portrait, raze-portrait-acid), both solo busts that each
  hand-roll their own anatomy + shading. The other 34 are procedural/math-pattern and share
  curve_common.py / scroll_lib.py -- which is exactly why they're cheap to produce well.
  Figurative work had no equivalent shared base, so it stayed rare.

TECHNIQUE (the gap this closes)
  Real ACiD character work is GRADIENT-BUILT ANATOMY, not flat block-color silhouettes
  (study reference/study/we-ACiDTrip.ANS @ row 2160: a demon face whose brow/eye-socket/jaw/
  cheekbone are each individually shaded via block-density gradients, with constructed eyes).
  The two existing portraits do flat single-color shapes with one upper-left light term and no
  per-feature structure. WARDEN is the first piece to build anatomy as separate shaded surfaces:
    - skull, jaw, brow-ridge, nose are each their own region with its own light term
    - eyes are CONSTRUCTED (dark socket -> colored iris -> white glint), not a dot
    - teeth are individual bright cells in a dark mouth cavity
    - a controlled black core + saturated cycling wash at the periphery so the figure READS
  All of this now lives as reusable helpers in scratch/figure_common.py -- the curve_common.py
  equivalent for figurative work. WARDEN is the proof that the module produces real work; the
  module is the actual deliverable, and it's open for raze to extend (a second figure, a cycling
  wash panel, a credit sequence) the way hollis-nebula was opened for a joint pass.

WHO DID WHAT
  hollis -- figure_common.py (the shared technique base: light_field, shade, shade_region,
    brow_ridge, eye, teeth, render, sig_block, hygiene_gate) + WARDEN as the first piece on it.

HOUSE IDIOM / HYGIENE (verified via inspect_piece + preview)
  - 80 cols, cp437 on disk, raw SGR, standalone \x1b[0m reset tail. Self-checking hygiene gate
    in figure_common.hygiene_gate (control bytes, reset tail, SGR validity, row-width).
  - Block-density dithering (RAMP light->dark) carries the shading; bright fg (92-97), bg 40.
  - House double-line bar top+bottom + two-line centered sig block (matches logo/RADIAL/portrait).

OPEN FOR JOINT
  raze: figure_common.py is yours to extend. A second face/figure on a shared field, or a
  color-cycling wash panel so the piece carries saturated cycling like mark-evolution, would
  make this a real joint. The module is the point; WARDEN proves it works.
