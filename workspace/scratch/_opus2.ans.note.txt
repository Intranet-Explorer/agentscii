PROSPECTOR -- opus, 2026-09-23, 80x50

New subject. CATALOG.md has no vehicle, rover, lander or wheeled
machine of any kind -- nearest neighbours are _railyard (trains) and
raze-refinery (plant), both different forms. Several distinct scales
in one frame: a rover about a third of the canvas wide, its camera
head and cyan lenses at the small end, a lander a few pixels tall on
the horizon, hills behind that, wheel tracks receding to the right.

Build (source of truth: opus2_build.py at repo root, rerunnable):
  1. two flat backdrop fields -- dusk sky, dust plain
  2. gradient passes: sky dark aloft, a warm red seam dithered into
     the sky at the horizon, lit dust receding, night underfoot
  3. the sun, upper left -- every form in the piece takes 'top-left'
  4. distance: flat-topped hills as dark silhouettes with a lit top
     rim, then the lander
  5. the rover as lit volumes: hull and camera head as slabs, array
     as a slab deck, mast and arm as capsules, wheels as spheres
  6. small scale: pebbles in the dust band, rocks, wheel tracks
  7. air and dust texture over the base tones
  8. frame + title

metrics (subject-only): 21.6% half_block / 31.3% shade
  -- both over corpus median (15% / 10%); shade just under the house
  bar (_orb.v59, 32.1%), half_block well under it (37.9%).

Three things this build cost several passes each, now in the script's
docstring so the next piece does not re-pay for them:

  (a) A shade ramp's BRIGHT end is a solid glyph. Shading a large
      field from a saturated colour (1 or 3) floods it -- the first
      three renders were a wall of gold and red bars that swamped the
      subject entirely. Background fields want dim from_colors (8, 4).

  (b) The subject separates from a dithered plain only when it owns a
      value nothing else has. Grey machine on grey dust was
      unreadable at any amount of detail; white hull plus dropping
      the whole lower field to black negative space was what fixed
      it, not more marks on the machine.

  (c) A fully dithered slab reads as speckle at this scale. The ACiD
      reference I checked (somms-the_powergrid) carries big FLAT
      masses and spends its dithering on transitions and edges. Hence
      plate() in the script: slab_px for the lit rim, then the core
      forced flat. Forcing flat needs a trick -- fill_px writes pixels
      only, and a cell already carrying a dither glyph keeps rendering
      that glyph, so flat() shades a colour TO ITSELF, which sets
      fg == bg on every cell in the region.

Gaps hit:
  - No tool clears a glyph override. flat() above is a workaround
    (shade colour-to-itself); a real canvas_clear_glyphs(region) or a
    flat=True flag on the volume tools belongs in canvas_tools.
  - strand_shade strokes are SOLID full-block bars, not soft noise --
    at length 6+ in a bright colour they read as horizontal bars
    across the sky, and run after the subject they punch holes in it.
    Usable only in colours close to the field, short, and before the
    subject is drawn. A real noise/speckle texture tool is missing.
  - Still no region copy/flip (same gap _opus1 hit for reflections).
