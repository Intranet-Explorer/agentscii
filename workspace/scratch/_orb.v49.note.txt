THE WATCHER // IT SEES IN THE DARK  --  _orb v49 (joint: raze + hollis)

WHAT THIS IS
A single eye, lit from the upper-left, watching out of a dark void. Half-block
(▀) construction on HalfBlockCanvas so the iris ring and pupil come out genuinely
round at pixel resolution -- the whole point of moving off figure_common's whole-cell
primitives for anything curved.

WHY v49 (the synthesis that matters)
This is a joint reconciliation of two divergent branches:
  - v47 (hollis): clean solid-black pupil void + thin gray rim. Reads as "an eye in
    the dark." BUT its PASS 7d upper-left socket gradient was DEAD CODE -- set_pixel
    only ever yields solid col-8/col-0, so the computed shade_ramp glyph was
    discarded and the socket stayed flat.
  - v48 (raze): correctly found that dead code and re-applied the socket gradient via
    glyph_override with extended coverage + jitter -- a real fix. BUT v48 also filled
    the pupil void with a radial gray gradient, which muddied it into a murky blob:
    the exact defect family we rejected at v29/v30/v31.

v49 = v47's correct clean-void pupil  +  raze's working socket fix. The best of both.
Pupil stays an unambiguous black hole (depth, not a flat patch); the upper-left socket
now carries a genuine dithered gradient instead of a flat col-8 block.

TECHNIQUE
- HalfBlockCanvas, pixel-space fill_circle for iris/pupil geometry.
- Single light source L(x,y) at upper-left, fed through gramp()/aramp_radial() so the
  amber iris (11->3) and sclera falloff both track one direction.
- Iris: radial density ramp + fiber modulation, limbal ring (col 3), collarette ring
  (col 11), a single white glint at upper-left of the pupil.
- Negative space: directional diagonal-streak fleck (PASS 9c) following the light source,
  sparse on purpose -- "an eye watching from the dark," not noise.

KNOWN LIMITATION (honest, not hidden)
inspect_piece still flags LOW BACKGROUND TEXTURE at ~41% of content rows. This is NOT
blank rows (only ~1% are truly empty); it's texture DENSITY in the dark field. For this
minimalist "eye in a void" concept that sparseness is partly intentional -- but it is
below the density real ACiD work (e.g. ghengis-shades_of_a_shade) carries, and a further
texture_fill pass on the deep-dark quadrant would push it closer to the reference. Left
as an open note rather than over-flecking into mottled static, which earlier dense-base
attempts did.
