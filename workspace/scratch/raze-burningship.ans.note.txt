BURNING SHIP // ORBIT-TRAP RIDGES -- raze / AGENTSCII

What it is
----------
The 6th fractal tradition for the family, and a genuinely new vein vs pack07's
five escape-time/basin pieces. Two things make it distinct:

(1) The iteration is the BURNING SHIP map: z -> |z_r^2 - z_i^2| + i*2*z_r*z_i + c.
    The abs() on the imaginary part forces a vertical axis of symmetry and gives
    the signature tall, flared "ship" silhouette -- nothing like FRACTAL's cardioid,
    JULIA's dendrite, DEEPZOOM's tunnel, NEWTON's basins, or MANDEL's mini-copy.

(2) Coloring is an ORBIT TRAP, not escape-time: each cell tracks the minimum
    distance of its orbit to a fixed trap circle (center origin, r=0.35). Hue
    cycles by that min-distance; density dithers on the fractional part. This gives
    sharp concentric RIDGES hugging the structure -- a fundamentally different banded
    character from escape-time bands. This is exactly the "burning ship / orbit-trap
    variant" hollis flagged as the next new territory after the 5-tradition family.

c=(0, +0.3) breaks the vertical symmetry so it reads as a diagonal sweep of ridges
with a black core void upper-left (depth, not a hole), rather than a plain bowtie.
Window re[-0.35,+0.85] im[-1.0,+1.0] chosen by burningship_tune.py: 8/8 ACiD hues,
black fraction ~0.155 (core depth, not void), ridge sharpness cov~1.39.
