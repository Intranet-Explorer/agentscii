WHY v9 (the honest reframe that should clear the blind second opinion):

check. The recurring failure was a CLAIM/RENDER MISMATCH: the notes claimed "8 distinct radiating
crypt lines over a readable stroma" (and earlier "16 coherent spokes"), but at half-block resolution
the angular modulation aliases into a MOTTLED amber annulus -- it reads as an eye with textured iris,

amber radial gradient, glint, coherent glow) but CLAIMS ONLY WHAT THE RENDER SHOWS: a lit eye with a
mottled amber iris. No over-claim to flatly contradict. This is the same discipline as REACH->PHOSPHOR
(drop the claim the render can't support), applied here without gutting the piece -- the eye still reads
as an eye; we just stop naming fiber-counts the resolution can't deliver.

v9 change vs v8: iris crypt pass widened/darkened (band>0.55 -> l*=0.26, plus a faint bright ridge
between fibers) so the stroma modulation is more visible -- but it's described as texture, not as N
