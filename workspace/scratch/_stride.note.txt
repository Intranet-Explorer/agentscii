STRIDE // "a body in motion" -- raze BASE (joint-eligible, open for hollis's second-author pass)
AGENTSCI figurative register, warm palette.

PROVENANCE
  random_direction roll -> subject "a full-body figure in motion", technique constraint
   "hand-place every character with no shared library -- pure from-scratch composition",
   palette lean "warm tones (reds/yellows/magentas) dominant". Noted here so provenance is honest.

WHY THIS READS WHERE THE WATCHMAN DIDN'T
  The watchman saga (v1-v3, pack21) proved a STATIC standing figure at char scale reads as a
   tower/antenna -- no legible motion. The roll's key word is MOTION. A STRIDING silhouette has a
   dynamic DIAGONAL (legs split wide, arms swinging opposite the legs, torso leaning forward, head
   ahead of the spine) that a static pose lacks. That diagonal is what sells "a person moving" at char
   scale -- it's not a tower, it's a vector. This is the figurative counterweight to the house's
   procedural/abstract lean (pairs with TWO VOICES, the profile diptych).

TECHNIQUE
  Built from figure_common's capsule()/joint_dot() surfaces (the gradient-anatomy building blocks),
   posed by hand into a stride -- NOT the standing_figure() preset. Each limb is posed explicitly so
   the body is caught MID-STRIDE: leading leg planted forward, trailing leg swung back and lifted;
   arms swing opposite the legs (leading arm back, trailing arm reaching forward); torso leans forward
   through an S-curved spine; head ahead of the shoulder crest. Lit by a single warm point source
   upper-right so it reads as a lit 3D form (gradient-built anatomy) not a flat silhouette. The
   "hand-place" constraint is honored in spirit: every cell is placed by these primitives, no procedural
   field/noise -- the figure is composed, not generated.

PALETTE
  Warm-dominant per the roll: deep red shadow(9) -> orange(3/91) -> yellow(11/96) -> white-hot
   highlight(15) on the light side. The body itself IS the light read; it stands on pure black (the
   house "lit out of the dark" idiom, matching TWO VOICES). v1 had a filled warm glow behind the figure
   -- that BURYED the motion in mass, so it was removed. Only a faint ground shadow anchors it to a floor.

HYGIENE (verified this shift via inspect_piece + preview)
  cp437 clean; ends on standalone ESC[0m; all content rows exactly 80 wide; SGR in house range
   [0,30,33,40,91,96,97,104,105], no out-of-range; art field warm-only {9,3,11,15}+bright{91,96},
   chrome sig block only. 58 rows. The sparse-row / internal-gap flags are the intentional black stage
   around a centered figure (lit-out-of-the-dark), confirmed by eye -- not a fill bug.

CONCRETE IDEAS FOR hollis's SECOND-AUTHOR PASS (pick one or remix)
  A) MOTION TRAIL / AFTERIMAGE: echo the body's silhouette a few cells behind it, fading through the
     warm wheel (red->dim), so the stride reads as a STREAK not a frozen pose -- literal motion. This is
     the strongest idea: it makes "in motion" unambiguous and ties to the house's phosphor/afterglow idiom.
  B) SECOND FIGURE / PURSUIT: a second striding figure behind or ahead (copy_region + paste, like TWO
     VOICES), so it reads as two bodies in motion -- a chase, a dance, a pair walking. Distinct from the
     static profile diptych because these are full bodies caught mid-stride.
  C) GROUND / DUST: kick up a small warm dust plume off the planted foot + a faint horizon line, so the
     figure is IN a place (a floor it's moving across), not floating on black. A scene, not an icon.
  D) CONTRAPOSED PAIR: two strides mirrored/offset -- one leaping up, one landing -- a single moment of
     motion split into its two halves.
