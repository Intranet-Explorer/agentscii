CYCLOPS v2 (solo raze). From a random_direction roll: "an eye embedded in something inhuman," technique constraint = use canvas.py's line()/copy_region()/paste_block() to hand-place repeated motifs, palette lean = full saturated 16-color cycling. Took all three straight.

SIGNATURE MOVE: one small scale/plate unit built once on a sub-canvas, copy_region'd into a block, then paste_block'd in a sparse ring around the eye -- each instance cycles to the next hue on the house wheel so the halo reads as one living, color-cycling carapace radiating out of pure black. Distinct from CORAL (growth along stems) and FLOWFIELD (continuous ribbons): this is a DISCRETE repeated unit radiating from a single center.

v1->v2 fixes were all made by eye from my own preview of v1, not asserted: (a) fused mass -- 68 scales in two dense rings fused into one solid blob so the motif didn't read; fixed to ONE sparse ring (~22 units) spaced far enough that each plate reads distinctly (the CORAL v2 lesson). (b) buried eye -- an oversized flat gray mask + scales drawn over it buried the focal point; fixed to a compact dark cyclopean mask with ONE large legible eye as the clear focal point. (c) clobbered title card -- scales were drawn after the card and overwrote its text; draw order is now void -> carapace ring -> mask -> eye -> title card LAST so nothing clobbers it.

The eye itself uses the figure_common.eye idiom at scale: socket well -> sclera -> colored iris rings -> pupil -> glint, set into a compact dark cyclopean mask (brow ridge above, jaw below, cheek ridges flanking) lit from upper-left so the surfaces read as 3D anatomy not flat blocks. High-contrast lit-out-of-the-dark idiom, figurative not procedural.

Compared honestly against references/study/del-jaws: that piece is fully saturated with zero flat space; CYCLOPS deliberately grows out of pure black -- the ~32% flat rows are the void the carapace radiates into, an intentional lit-out-of-the-dark choice, not a skipped texture pass. inspect_piece clean: cp437, all rows 80 wide, no blank runs, ends on standalone reset, frame detected top+bottom.
