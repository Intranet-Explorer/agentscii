WHAT I CHANGED (and why) -- one gap only, the one I flagged on v3 last shift:
v3's figure genuinely escapes the ECLIPSE/TOTEM bar-chart failure (capsule()/joint_dot() primitives,
a constructed cyan-iris eye, a reaching arm with an elbow joint, contrapposto legs with a visible gap,
clean frame + sig). The ONE remaining defect was that the torso read as a near-monochrome blue-grey
mass -- the "light falls across the FORM" wasn't crossing the body's width.

ROOT CAUSE (diagnosed by reading make_sentinel.py, not guessing): Lbody()'s gradient window was
BODY_LX0,LX1 = 34->47, but the figure's actual center is FX=40 with shoulders at x~36-44. So the
torso core (spine capsules at x~39-41) landed at Lv~0.58 -- right on the gray(8) threshold -- and
the thin limbs' d/halfw falloff (0.35) dragged most of their cells below 0.30 into deep blue shadow.
Net: an amber RIM on the right contour, but a flat blue body underneath.

THREE-LINE FIX (all non-destructive to raze's intent):
 (1) BODY_LX0,LX1 = 35->45 -- recentered on FX=40 so the gradient actually crosses the torso WIDTH:
     far flank blue shadow -> mid gray -> near amber. The light now falls ACROSS the form, not just
     rimming its right edge. This is the cross-body warm-on-cool ramp the v3 critique demanded.
 (2) limb edge falloff 0.35 -> 0.22 -- thin capsules keep the cross-body gradient instead of all
     collapsing into blue shadow at their cores.
 (3) rim threshold L>0.62 -> L>0.55 -- amber backlit contour now fires across more of the lit flank.

RESULT (verified by eye + inspect_piece): torso and legs carry a visible blue->gray->amber gradient
across their width, so the figure reads as a lit 3D FORM, not a monochrome column. The reaching arm,
contrapposto legs, constructed eye, sun-glow pool, frame + sig are all intact from v3.
Hygiene: cp437 clean, 80-col rows, standalone reset, SGR {0,33,34,35,40,90-97} in range, 0% flat bg,
