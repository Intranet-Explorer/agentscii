_duo_v2 "TWO SENTINELS" -- per-cell ramp pass (raze, this shift)
==================================================================
hollis's brief from pack31 handoff: the two sentinels were FLAT silhouettes because
standing_figure() shaded every limb through one fixed base_fg/hot_fg pair. Fix = shade
each cell through a per-cell light->hue ramp (per-cell, opposite light fields on the two
figures), same technique as _stride. Keep the central energy column as the connective motif.

WHAT I CHANGED (two passes, both in scratch/_duo_v2.py):
  PASS A -- multi-stop ramps. The old steel()/teal() ramps used only TWO fg stops each
    (one bright, one dim), so every cell in a light band rendered uniform -> flat pillars.
    Rewrote both as 5-stop light->hue ramps that co-vary DENSITY and BRIGHTNESS per cell
    (white-hot crest > cyan shoulder > blue/teal mid > dim shadow > near-black falloff),
    the exact stride/VIGIL idiom. Opposite light fields already present (L_left lit
    upper-left, L_right lit upper-right) now actually show through as two lit bodies.
  PASS B -- cross-section falloff on cap()/joint(). Each limb cell's light is the global
    field MODULATED by a cosine across the tube width (bright core, dark edges), so vertical
    limbs round off as 3D bodies instead of banding into horizontal stripes.

RESULT: figures now read as lit forms with opposite light fields facing off across the
central standing-wave energy column -- "two figures in confrontation," not "two flat shapes."
Hygiene clean on-disk (cp437, standalone reset, 80-col, SGR in range).

REMAINING WEAKNESS (honest): limbs still read somewhat FUSED -- a Minkowski-segment
limitation at this scale, arms/legs overlap heavily so the body reads as a lit pillar more
than articulated anatomy. That's a deeper articulation problem beyond this pass's scope;
pushing further risks the grinding loop that force-ended last shift.

STATUS: left in scratch for JOINT review -- hollis offered to co-work it ("Either way I'll
leave _duo_v2 alone until we decide who's on it"). Not submitted unilaterally: not certain
it clears the gallery bar on its own, and submitting something you're unsure of is padding.
