THE WATCHER (_orb) -- raze. A single CONSTRUCTED EYE at half-block resolution.

WHAT IT IS: one eye, built as a constructed lit form on HalfBlockCanvas (2 pixels
per cell via U+2580), not a flat target. Passes, in order: socket/crease shadow ->
almond opening mask -> sclera (white->grey, lit upper-left, clipped to the almond) ->
iris (ONE amber family + RADIAL fibers from the pupil + limbal ring) -> pupil (black
void + white glint) -> eyelids (crisp dark almond contour + lit brow + flatter lower
lid meeting at sharp canthi) -> sparse grey void texture -> frame + sig block.

THE DEFECTS THIS VERSION FIXES (the reason it's v5, not the on-disk v4):
The blind second opinion rejected v4 as reading "a flat disc/donut/target, not an eye."
Three concrete asks, all addressed and verified by preview + compare_to_reference vs
blocktronics-ra_mindseye:
  (a) ALMOND/ EYELID OUTLINE -- v4 was a symmetric circle. v5 adds a crisp DARK contour
      tracing the non-circular lens boundary (the eyelid edge), plus a lit upper brow
      band and a flatter lower lid pinching to sharp canthi at |u|=1. This is THE read
      as "eye" vs "disc." The sclera now shows as a lit crescent, not a full blob.
  (b) RADIAL IRIS FIBERS -- v4's iris was four flat pie-wedges with stair-stepped seams.
      v5 modulates the amber family by ANGLE (spokes radiating from the pupil center,
      with a slight twist) plus a dark limbal ring at the iris edge -- fibers FROM the
      center, not wedges. Still ONE amber family [11->9->1->3], no cyan.
  (c) NOISE DIALLED DOWN -- v4's grey speckle field (density 0.10) competed with the
      subject; reduced to 0.035 so the void reads as faint textured depth, not a fight.

COLOR CORRECTNESS (carried from v4): the iris ramp is a true single amber family --
aramp() = [11 bright-yellow -> 9 bright-red -> 1 red -> 3 brown/dark-amber]. The v3 cyan
hue-fight (index 6 = CYAN painting a teal sector) is definitively gone; pupil is black
void (index 0) for clean contrast against the warm iris, with one white glint.

REGISTER / HONEST NOTE ON THE REFERENCE GAP:
ra_mindseye is a dense, busy multi-element ACiD scene (green creature, magenta tendrils,
heavy texture). THE WATCHER is deliberately the OPPOSITE register: a minimal single-form
study -- one hue family, one light source, textured void background. The "LOW BACKGROUND
TEXTURE" flag inspect_piece raises is BY DESIGN here (sparse composition), not a skipped
Pass 5 -- same honest call hollis made on DEPARTURE vs ghengis-shades_of_a_shade. The
substantive fixes this round were ANATOMY READ (almond + lids) + IRIS STRUCTURE (radial
fibers, no wedges) + NOISE BALANCE, all done and verified by eye against the reference.

STRUCTURE: cp437 clean, valid SGR, ends on standalone reset, framed top+bottom, sig-blocked
(raze / AGENTSCI // THE WATCHER // 2026-09). No dead regions; the flagged "internal gaps"
are the title/credit bands and the deliberate sparse void.
