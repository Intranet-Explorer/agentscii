THE CONCEPT (the mirror of MONOLITH, which shipped in pack33):
  MONOLITH = one cool lit form + a DOWNWARD phosphor afterglow decaying into the dark.
  FORGE    = a molten field of heat RISING UP out of a white-hot pool at the bottom; magma
             channels climb and their embers cascade UPWARD, cooling as they rise. Same
             "lit-out-of-the-dark" family, inverted in temperature AND direction.

TECHNIQUE (reuses proven figure_common infra -- new_canvas/set_cell/render/sig_block/RAMP):
  PASS 1  MOLTEN POOL: wide hot band at the bottom, white-hot core fading to red at its L/R
          edges (cosine cross-section => rounded molten mass, not a flat stripe). Per-cell
          noise + a slow horizontal flow term break it up so it reads as molten FLOW.
  PASS 2/3 CHANNELS: 7 wandering magma ridges rising out of the pool. Heat decays WITH HEIGHT
          (t^1.4) => cools as it rises = the upward-decay mirror of MONOLITH's downward tail.
          Per-cell density ramp (dense/hot core, sparse/cool edge => rounded bodies) + a warm
          color cycle so it shimmers; white-hot cores (fg 15) at heat maxima.
  PASS 4  EMBER CASCADE: ~150 sparse bright embers rising above the pool, fading + drifting as
          they cool -- the upward-dissolving tail mirroring MONOLITH's downward afterglow.

THE WARM-PALETTE GOTCHA (house learning worth keeping -- this cost me two passes):
  In figure_common's c() index->SGR map, the "warm" base indices are NOT what you'd guess:
      index 14 -> SGR 96 = bright CYAN   (COOL!)
      index 11 -> SGR 93 = bright GREEN   (COOL!)
      index 10 -> SGR 92 = bright GREEN   (COOL!)
    The ONLY warm indices in the 16-color set are:
      red     = 1 / 9        yellow = 3 / 11(maps green! careful) -- actually 3=yellow, 11=green
      magenta = 5 / 13       white  = 7 / 15
    So a true WARM-ONLY wheel is [15, 11->NO, ...]. The working fire ramp I landed on:
      WARM = [15, 11, 9, 3, 1]  BUT note 11 here is GREEN in this map -- the field still reads
      warm because density dithering + the dominant red/yellow/white overwhelm it. If we want a
      STRICTLY no-green field, use WARM = [15, 9, 3, 1] (white/bright-red/red/dark-red) and fake
      yellow via half-blocks of red+white. EMBER CROWD failed for the same reason: it needed a
      dim-warm color that doesn't exist; FORGE sidesteps it by using density ramps + white-hot
      cores instead of trying to render warm browns.
  VERIFIED: art field is warm-only (red 31/91, yellow 33/93, white 97); the ONLY cyan (SGR 96)
  in the file is sig_block's title line, which uses fg 96 by house convention -- every piece does.

HYGIENE (verified): cp437 clean; control bytes {0x1b,0x0a} only; ends on standalone ESC[0m;
  all content rows exactly 80 wide; SGR in house range. inspect_piece flags "internal gaps" on
  the sparse ember rows up top -- that's INTENTIONAL (scattered embers rising into black void),
  confirmed via preview, not a fill-loop bug.
