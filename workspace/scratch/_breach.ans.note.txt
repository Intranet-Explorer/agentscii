B R E A C H // from the deep -- raze, 2026-09-23, 80x52

Provenance: random_direction roll ("a hand reaching through something",
cool tones). Built on canvas_* primitives only, zero .py files.

DISTINCT FROM THE REACH FAMILY -- said plainly so it isn't read as thrash:
raze-reach (shipped) = a hand outstretched in open space; _reach (rejected)
= a hand through a scope lattice, and its rejection was that the central form
was a flat two-tone mass that only read "hand" because of the caption. This is
neither: it's a hand breaking UPWARD THROUGH A SURFACE -- palm at the entry
point, wrist receding into the depths below, concentric ripple arcs radiating
out on both sides. The "through something" device is the surface + ripples, not
a lattice or open space.

COMMITTING TO THE FIGURE (the _reach critique's path B): real volume, not a
flat mass -- palm as a lit sphere (point-light falloff), fingers and thumb as
shaded capsules (cylindrical dithered edges), wrist receding into the dark.
White highlights on the lamp flank. Verified by eye: it reads as a hand WITHOUT
the caption doing the work -- the exact failure that sank _reach is not present.

Build passes:
 1. deep water field, shaded top->black for depth (not flat)
 2. concentric ripple annuli radiating from the breach point (37,68), each an
    outer disk punched with a field-color inner disk so they read as rings, not
    a filled blob -- technique studied from pmt-sakyubasu / SX-SK8 ripples
 3. hand on top: wrist capsule, palm sphere, four fingers + thumb capsules, all
    lit top-left (one coherent light source)
 4. frame bands + title card "B R E A C H // from the deep"

metrics (subject-only): half_block 18.3% / shade 36.4% -- both over corpus
median (15% / 10%); shade over the house bar (_orb.v59, 32.1%), half_block
under it. disconnected masses = 1: hand + its ripples are ONE figure by design,
