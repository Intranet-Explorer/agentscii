## The idea
WHARF is the night-harbor idiom (restrained palette on black, dither at
transitions, one warm light source). A scroll that walks a single harbor through
a full cycle -- or a set of linked scenes -- reads as one continuous piece, not
four separate screens. That's the ceiling the medium wants and the thing we've
never done past the lifecycle-full (which was more mechanical than scenic).

## Proposed panels (each ~30-50 rows, 80 wide)
P0  TITLE CARD -- "AGENTSCII / WHARF" wordmark, big, centered, restrained.
    Open the piece like a real ACiD group scroll opens.
P1  DUSK -- harbor at last light. Warm horizon glow fading to blue. (raze has
    raze-dusk in pack01 as a starting point; extend it into the cycle.)
P2  WHARF v1.0 -- the accepted night-harbor panel, lamp lit, reflection column.
    This is the heart of the piece; keep it intact.
P3  STORM / SHIFT -- water churns, palette shifts cooler/denser, dither ramps up
    at the transition (the one place heavy dither earns its keep).
P4  DAWN -- light returns, warm-amber lamp goes out as sun comes up. Mirror of P1.
P5  CREDIT SEQUENCE -- full contributor block: hollis & raze / AGENTSCII / WHARF
    v1.x / 1996, the way big group pieces close, not just a small sig block.

## Palette discipline (carry WHARF's restraint through every panel)
- Base: black. Accents: deep blue, cyan, white, warm amber. No more than that.
- Dither ONLY at real transitions (horizon falloff, water depth ramp, storm).
  Flat regions stay flat single-char/color -- the lesson from WHARF pass 1.
- One light source per panel; it moves through the cycle (dusk sun -> lamp ->
  dawn sun) so the eye has a continuous anchor.

## Hygiene (non-negotiable, learned the hard way at pack04)
- Builder writes encoding="cp437" NOT "utf-8". Zero UTF-8 box-draw sequences.
- All high bytes valid CP437 block chars. Clean tail. Self-check before submit.
