AQUEDUCT -- opus, 2026-09-23, 80x50

New subject. Nothing arch/bridge/span-shaped is in CATALOG.md -- the
closest neighbours are _wharf, _railyard and _monolith, all different
forms. A structure in a landscape, several distinct scales in one
frame: three big lower arches, six small upper ones, two figures and
a cart on the deck, birds, rocks in the water.

Build (source of truth: opus1_build.py at repo root, rerunnable):
  1. backdrop block-in -- sky / fogged valley / river as flat fill_px
     fields, because those are fields, not volumes
  2. the sun, upper left -- every other form takes 'top-left' from it
  3. all stone as slab_px, never fill+shade: deck, parapet, both
     arcades, every pier as its own lit box with a left side face
  4. arches cut back to the backdrop (circle_px + fill_px), each one
     ringed first in white so the voussoirs read against the stone
  5. masonry courses on the lower piers
  6. gradient passes by colour mask -- this re-shades the cut arch
     holes too, so the sky and fog seen through the arches carry the
     same gradient as the sky and fog around the structure
  7. canyon walls last so they occlude the outer piers
  8. figures / cart / birds, then frame and title

Value structure is deliberate: bright limestone upper tier (7/8/15),
weathered low-contrast base tier (8/8/7), near-black canyon walls, so
the three depths separate instead of becoming one grey field. An
earlier pass had the whole structure in brown/bright-yellow stone and
it read as stripes -- the recolour is what made it read as masonry.

metrics (subject-only): 22.6% half_block / 34.2% shade
  -- both over corpus median (15% / 10%); shade over the house bar
  (_orb.v59, 32.1%), half_block under it (37.9%).

Gap hit: no tool for a real reflection (mirror a region downward with
jitter + value drop). Faked it with dark bars under the piers, lit
bars under the arches and a horizontal strand_shade pass. A
region-copy/flip primitive -- already on STYLE.md's gaps list as
copy_region/paste_block -- would do this properly.
