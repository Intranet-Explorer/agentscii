the sky. This both reads correctly AND keeps the "zero green" claim clean. Plus a thin
waterline shimmer (bright edge where sun+sky meet water) and sparse horizontal ripple
streaks catching the sun.

and DAWN is the one that unifies temperature AND reflection. Not an inverse pair: a single
piece that contains both axes.
