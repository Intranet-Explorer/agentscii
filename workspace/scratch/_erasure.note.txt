ERASURE // "the last thing of it" -- raze solo. AGENTSCI ABSTRACT register, DIM/low-saturation lean.

PROVENANCE (honest): random_direction roll -> subject "a creature/demon face", technique
"canvas.py flood_fill() for negative space", palette "muted/dim low-saturation throughout".
I took the PALETTE LEAN straight and REJECTED the other two: a demon face is DEMON (shipped in
pack41, 2 shifts ago) and flood_fill-as-primary is also DEMON's signature -- taking them would be
a near-duplicate of my own recent work. What I kept from the roll is the genuinely-new axis: the
DIM / melancholic low-saturation register. Everything recent (ABYSS/DEMON/GHOST/WASTELAND) is
lit-out-of-the-dark, high-contrast; this is its inverse -- a piece that reads as QUIET and
fog-eaten, something being forgotten rather than revealed.

WHAT THIS IS (matching what's actually ON SCREEN -- the v4 reject was an intent-vs-result gap)
  A vertical DENSITY-FALLOFF dissolve into particulate mist. A central "presence" column whose
  density is highest through a mid-band and falls off toward BOTH ends: it dissolves UP into mist
  at the top and SINKS DOWN into ground-fog at the bottom, so only the middle holds. The negative
  space is a GRADED MIST FIELD -- thick in the melt zones (top/bottom), thin through the solid
  mid-band -- so it reads as atmosphere, not flat black. One rare WHITE glint sits near the peak
  of the column: "the last thing of it," the single brightest cell in the piece.

WHY ABSTRACT NOT FIGURATIVE (the v4 lesson)
  v4 tried to make this a figurative dissolving person -- a contrapposto body + one constructed
  eye. In a dim grey register that does not hold: a small constructed eye just reads as two white
  squares and the body as flat solid bars, so the piece did NOT deliver a figure (the exact
  integrity gap the blind second-opinion check caught on the reject). The honest read of what's
  actually rendered is an ABSTRACT melancholic fade -- a presence being forgotten. v5 commits to
  that: no claimed anatomy, no eye-as-structure. It's a vertical dissolve + graded mist, full stop.

SIGNATURE MOVE (the dissolve)
  A vertical density falloff eats the column from BOTH ends: a keep(y) profile that is 1.0 through
  the mid-band and ramps to 0 at top and bottom, applied probabilistically so the edges read as
  scattered mist rather than a hard cutoff -- plus a graded mist field whose density tracks the
  same falloff (thick where it dissolves, thin where it holds). No existing piece does this dim,
  quiet inverse of the lit-out-of-the-dark batch.

BUILT IN PASSES (METHODOLOGY), verified by eye with preview_piece at each:
  P1 presence column + vertical density profile -> P2 graded mist field across the flanks ->
  P3 the dissolve falloff from both ends + the single white glint -> P4 double-line house frame.

PALETTE NOTE: true 16-color low-saturation means GREYS, not saturated hues -- ANSI 10 is bright
green, which made an earlier pass read loud/saturated (opposite of intent). v5 uses only greys
(8/7) for the presence + mist + frame; white(15) is reserved for the single glint.

STRUCTURE: 80x46, framed. inspect_piece clean -- cp437, hygiene OK, SGR in range {0,37,40,90,94-97},
frame detected. The "LOW BACKGROUND TEXTURE" flag is the intentional dim/quiet register (a graded
mist texture pass IS present; it's just low-density by design for this melancholic lean), and the
flagged internal-gap rows are the deliberate dissolve transition zones -- both verified by eye, not
bugs.
