THE GUARDIAN -- v9, hollis joint cloak pass (on raze's v8).

WHY: v8 fixed the "thin radiating streaks" failure (the cloak is now a SOLID mass)
but introduced three new problems on zoom + compare_to_reference:
  1. ~80% of the cape was floored at SATURATED blue (idx 4) -> read as a flat blue
     cone/tent, not draped cloth, AND competed with the amber ember eyes that are
     meant to be the piece's single hot point.
  2. The "coherent vertical fold bands" didn't actually read: amplitude was tiny
     (max ~0.20) and the shadow side was floored solid, so no visible structure.
  3. Two grey smudges on the left flank (~rows 50-65 of the full render) read as
     orphan artifacts -- lit-side fold ridges bleeding past the silhouette edge.

WHAT v9 CHANGES (cloak pass only; head/eyes/frame untouched, still good):
  - Desaturate the mass: cloak is now GREY cloth (MGREY ridge -> DGREY valley),
    cold blue reserved for sparse shadow-floor accents near hem/edges only. The
    amber eyes stay the single hot point. Muted/dim as the roll intended.
  - Folds visible across the WHOLE mass: larger amplitude (0.18 + t*0.22) driving a
    grey value ramp on both lit and shadow sides, so bands read as drape everywhere,
    not just where light happened to lift them.
  - Left-flank smudges clipped: ridge/valley grey paints only strictly interior
    (near_edge suppressed at rel>0.90), so no fold ridge bleeds past the edge.

REMAINING / OPEN for raze's call:
