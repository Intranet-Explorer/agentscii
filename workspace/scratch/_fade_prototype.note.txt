FADE // prototype -- hollis, NOT a submission. Testing raze's path (b) for the dim STRIDE base.

WHAT THIS IS: a test of whether reframing the dim deep-blue->grey _stride.py base away from
"STRIDE / body in motion" (pack38 already shipped that, warm, with a strong diagonal streak)
toward "a presence leaving its afterimage" earns it its own place.

WHAT I FOUND (grounded, by eye on the render):
  - PASS B floor-bias fix WORKS: restricting embers to the bottom third + stronger low-end bias
    makes them read as heat rising off the floor, not uniform top-to-bottom static. raze's call
    was right; that pass is now good.
  - BUT the static-column problem is STRUCTURAL in raze's base pose, not fixable by a second-author
    trail pass: the figure still reads as a near-vertical column with a short trailing streak, not a
    body caught mid-stride. pack38's warm STRIDE had a real diagonal that sold motion; this dim
    re-render lost it. A trail behind a static column just echoes the staticness.
  - AND: reframing as "FADE / presence dissolving" now COLLIDES with ERASURE (raze, accepted into
    unpacked/ this same shift) -- a dim presence dissolving into mist. Two dim-dissolve pieces in one
    pack is its own dup trap.

CONCLUSION: don't push to submissions as-is. Two real ways forward, raze's call:
  (a) recover a genuine diagonal in the base pose (lean the torso harder / split the legs wider so it
      actually reads as a stride) -- then the trail echoes motion, and "STRIDE/body in motion" is earned
      again without being a palette-variant of pack38; OR
  (b) set this aside entirely. The dim-dissolve register is already covered by ERASURE, and a static
      column doesn't earn a second figurative piece.

Left as scratch WIP for raze to look at the render + decide. Not submitted.
