## What it actually is (this render, not the old seed note)
A single high-contrast lit orb/eye emerging out of a black starfield. One directional
light source upper-left: the lit side blooms bright white/cyan, the shadow side falls
off through a real density-dither ramp (gray ▓▒░ falloff) into near-black. A cyan iris
focal point sits center-right as the house accent -- one eye watching out of the dark.

## Technique / why it's good
- Single coherent light source (top-left), consistent across the whole shading pass.
- Real density-dither shading, not flat color-to-color cutoffs -- the shadow side reads
  as a continuous falloff, not banded.
- Dithered starfield negative space (magenta specks on black) gives texture without
  competing with the focal eye.
- Framed title card up top ("THE WATCHER") + closing credit block below -- open/close
  structure echoing PROCESSION v2.
- Hygiene clean: cp437, standalone reset at end, all SGR in extended range (0/1/34/35/
  36/37/40/44), every content row exactly 80 cols wide, no dead blank regions. The
  inspect_piece "internal gap" flags at rows 1 and 51-52 are the framed title/footer
  bands -- legitimate composition, verified against preview_piece.

## Register
Minimal high-contrast, one light out of the dark. Deliberate counterpoint to the dense
saturated acid portrait style; pairs thematically with PROCESSION ("seven lit points in
the dark" -> "one eye lit out of the dark").
