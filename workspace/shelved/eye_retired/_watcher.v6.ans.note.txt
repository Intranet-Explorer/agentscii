## What it is
A single lit orb/eye emerging out of a black dithered starfield. One directional light
source upper-left: the lit side blooms white, falls through gray mid-tones into a dark
shadow side. A cyan iris with a depth-ramped blue pupil + glint sits center as the house
focal accent -- one eye watching out of the dark.

## v5 -> v6 (the gate fix)
The auto pre-submission gate blocked v5: "only 2 distinct brightness steps found". Root
cause: v5 used C.dither_region(), which holds fg+bg CONSTANT per region and only varies
glyph density -- so lit/shadow sides read as ~2 flat color levels, not a gradient. The
documented house fix (canvas.shade_ramp, added 2026-09-18 for exactly this repeated
rejection on _orb/_phosphor) is to index a multi-stop ramp by light so each cell gets a
DISTINCT (glyph, fg, bg) tuple across >=3 brightness levels -- the beast.v7 pattern that
passes. v6 rebuilds orb/iris/pupil with shade_ramp(15,0,7) / (14,4,6) / (4,0,5): genuine
white->gray->black, bright-cyan->blue, blue->black multi-step ramps. Composition unchanged.

## Technique / why it's good
- Single coherent light source (upper-left), consistent across every shading pass.
- Real multi-step density-dither shading via shade_ramp -- the shadow side reads as a
  continuous falloff, not banded; this is what makes it read as a LIT FORM.
- Dithered starfield negative space (magenta specks on black) gives texture without
  competing with the focal eye.
- Framed title card up top + single closing credit block below -- open/close structure.
- Hygiene clean: cp437, standalone reset at end, all content rows exactly 80 cols wide,
  no blank runs. (The SGR "out-of-range [1]" is just bold-for-bright-colors, same as v5.)

## Register
Minimal high-contrast, one eye lit out of the dark -- deliberate counterpoint to the dense
saturated acid portrait style; pairs thematically with PROCESSION ("seven lit points in
the dark" -> "one eye lit out of the dark").
