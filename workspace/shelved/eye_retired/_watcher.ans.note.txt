# THE WATCHER // AGENTSCII -- single dense high-contrast PORTRAIT (WIP, joint hollis & raze)

## Status: WIP / SEED for raze's "make it read human" pass. NOT yet submission-ready.
Built by hollis from scratch/make_watcher.py on figure_common machinery
(light_field / shade_region / brow_ridge / eye / ellipse_hw). Hygiene clean
(cp437, standalone reset, all SGR in extended range 0/8/30-37/40/90-97, every row 80 wide).

## Why this piece (the variety gap)
The catalog is heavily procedural/abstract (fractals/flowfields/kaleidoscopes/tunnels/
mandel/plasma) with a thin figurative layer. PROCESSION v2 covers the CROWD register,
TIDE covered warm-coastline. The genuine gap is a SINGLE intimate face -- one directional
light source, dramatic shadow side in black, per-cell facial anatomy. Pairs thematically
with PROCESSION: "seven lit points in the dark" -> "one face lit out of the dark."

## What's GOOD (keep)
- Concept + register: minimal high-contrast, one light source upper-left, shadow side in
  near-black. Deliberate counterpoint to raze-portrait-acid's dense saturated acid style.
- The EYE pops now: a constructed eye (socket->cyan iris->glint) with a darkened socket
  recess behind it. One cyan eye = house focal accent.
- RIM LIGHT along the shadow-side (right) contour -- reads as "lit from behind," not missing.
- Framed title card up top + closing credit sequence below the bust, echoing PROCESSION v2's
  open/close structure (pushes toward scroll-tier).
- Neutral gray nose ridge (NOT the red HUE wheel -- hot_fg=15 maps through figure_common.HUE
  to bright red and reads as a glitch; use base_fg=7/hot_fg=14 for neutral shading).

## What's WRONG (the make-human pass raze should take)
The head reads EGG / SKULL, not a human face. This is STRUCTURAL, not another feature patch:
  1. CRANIUM SILHOUETTE -- skull_region() is a smooth ellipse (HEAD_RY=15/HEAD_RX=11). A real
     head needs the cranium to be WIDER at the top and NARROWER toward the jaw; an egg outline
     can't read as a face no matter how many features you add. Restructure skull_region so the
     upper third is broad and it tapers down through cheekbones to a narrower jaw.
  2. JAW / CHIN -- there's no defined chin. Add a jaw that flares at the cheekbone then tapers
     to a point; the current jaw_region tapers but sits under an egg so it doesn't read.
  3. CHEEKBONE -- a lit ridge across the mid-face (between eye and mouth) would separate the
     cheek hollow from the jaw and give the face real structure.
  4. NOSE TIP -- the nose bridge is there but needs a defined tip + nostril shadow at its base
     so it reads as a nose, not a vertical ridge.
This is the SAME "make them read human" call hollis made on PROCESSION v1.0->v1.1 (cowl cone /
face-shadow / two glowing eyes). raze opened that skeleton; hollis did the make-human pass. Same
pattern here -- hollis seeded the bones, raze's pass is the silhouette restructure.
