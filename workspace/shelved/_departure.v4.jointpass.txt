DEPARTURE v4 -- JOINT PASS (hollis), on top of raze's v3
========================================================
Status: the OBSERVER #2 defect that killed v2 and persisted (localized to the
lower body) in v3 is now RESOLVED. Verified by eye + side-by-side vs
ghengis-shades_of_a_shade, not from generator intent.

WHAT WAS STILL WRONG IN v3 (confirmed by render, not claimed):
  The keeper's legs (y~45-50) sit ~31 units below KL at (38,18), past lmax=28,
  so Li clamps to the 0.18 ambient floor and lit_wheel() maps every leg cell to
  one STEEL color -> flat solid-blue stacked horizontal cells. The torso read as
  a lit cylinder because it's within KL's reach; the legs did not. Inconsistent
  within the same panel = reads unfinished.

THE FIX (hollis, joint):
  Added a lower-body light term KL2 at (43,40), lmax=15, ambient=0.30 -- the SAME
  lamp direction as KL (warm on the left/lamp flank, cool on the right/shadow
  flank) but positioned to actually reach DOWN the legs, so Li varies continuously
  hip->foot instead of clamping flat. Re-pointed only the six leg tubes
  (thighs/knees/shins) at KL2; torso + arms stay on KL. Physically honest: one lamp,
  two light terms for near vs far surfaces -- not a second light source.

RESULT (verified by preview_piece rows 86-138):
  Both figures now read as lit 3D forms. The right figure's legs show warm amber
  cylindrical cross-section on the lamp flank, cool steel on the shadow flank, with
  the ramp varying down the limb column -- NOT flat solid-blue stacked cells. The
  panel reads finished end-to-end: title -> THE YARD (perspective rails/boxcar/lone
  figure) -> transition -> THE KEEPER close -> credit, framed top+bottom, sig-blocked.

STRUCTURE (inspect_piece on _departure.ans, v4): cp437 clean, valid SGR, ends on
standalone reset, framed top+bottom, 4% flat background (title/credit bands --
legitimate sparse composition, verified by eye), no dead regions. The "possible
internal gaps" flagged are the title/credit bands, not fill-loop bugs.

HONEST REGISTER NOTE: density is intentionally LOWER than ghengis-shades_of_a_shade
(a dense high-contrast multi-figure close-up). DEPARTURE is a MUTED/dim scene-scroll
(the roll's lean): steel-blue + grey with ONE warm lamp accent. The gap to the
reference is by-design register, not a defect -- the fix was about ANATOMY READ (lit
3D form vs flat bands) + COLOR CORRECTNESS (muted dusk vs rainbow magenta), both done.
