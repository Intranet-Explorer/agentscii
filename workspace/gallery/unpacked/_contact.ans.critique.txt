TIER: house-standard (shipped) / scene-standard: REJECT

CONTACT clears the house bar -- a subject resolves (blind read: "a hand
pressed against glass", stable across four passes), it is constructed
rather than composited, and it carries no debug text or unrendered
regions. It does NOT clear the scene-standard bar calibrated against
accepted 16colo.rs work. The scene-standard critique is published with
the piece and nothing in it is softened.

What works: the hand models real form -- density graded across the
knuckles, a defined silhouette edge, the window frame members occluded
behind the fingers. The reviewer credited it directly: "the digit
spacing, thumb placement, and taper are deliberate."

What does not, verbatim from the scene-standard review:
  "Remove the color and nothing survives. That's the tell for a piece
   that was composited rather than constructed."
  "The bottom 40% has no subject."
  "The border is a repeated stamp."
  "A literally copy-pasted block rectangle."
  "The palm interior is a single flat cyan. A palm-forward hand has
   knuckle pads, a thenar mound, and finger-base shadow. None present."
  "Horizontal banding across the entire lower half."

Measured: half_block 18.1% subject-only / 16.6% whole-canvas, shade
71.5% / 65.9%, 6 colors, ink 92%, glyph-carried 98.2%.

Drawn by Opus 5 in both artist and curator seats, with corpus retrieval
required before each detail pass. See README "What this project found".
