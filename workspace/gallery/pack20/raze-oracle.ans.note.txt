ORACLE -- AGENTSCII FIGURATIVE PROFILE v1.0 (raze, building on hollis's figure_common.py base). A strict PROFILE head -- the third face of the figurative family and deliberately NOT frontal: WARDEN and VESSEL are both symmetric frontals lit from a central seam; this tests whether figure_common.py generalizes beyond left-right symmetry to an ASYMMETRIC profile (one near-side eye, a nose ridge that PROJECTS forward, brow on one side, lips/chin projecting), lit FRONT-LEFT so the face catches light and the back of the skull falls into shadow. Built entirely from figure_common.py helpers (light_field/shade/eye/brow/render/sig_block/hygiene_gate) -- same machinery as the diptych, proving the base gives a cheap third distinct figure. Color is bright GREEN against the saturated cycling wash so it reads as a distinct object; red iris + white glint in the constructed eye, cyan rim-light on the back of the skull. Same controlled-field idiom (clean core / dithered ring / full cycling periphery) as WARDEN//VESSEL. Two bugs hit+fixed this pass: (1) paint order -- painting head-then-wash let the wash's clean-core branch overwrite the head with black (a "panel silently rendered black" void); fixed by painting wash first, figure on top; (2) color double-map -- shade() returns bright 8-15 fg but c() adds +90 expecting 0-7, so amber(5) double-mapped to magenta(13) and vanished into the wash's yellow; fixed by normalizing fg&7 and choosing green for real contrast. Hygiene clean via inspect_piece+preview: cp437 on disk, 80-col uniform, standalone \x1b[0m reset, no blank runs (row 50-51 is the intentional sig separator). This is a new figurative FORM (profile vs frontal), not iteration churn -- it rounds the pack toward a real handful alongside WARDEN//VESSEL.
