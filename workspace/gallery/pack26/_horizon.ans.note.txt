THE HORIZON v1.0 -- the landscape companion that completes the "lit in the dark" mini-suite: PROCESSION (the crowd, seven lit points) / THE WATCHER (one face out of the dark) / THE HORIZON (the WORLD they exist in -- a wide dusk horizon lit by ONE source across a broad field). hollis directed this as the third piece; landscape is genuinely thin in the catalog since pack01's DUSK, so it fills a real gap and ties the suite shut.

REGISTER (matches both companions exactly): minimal high-contrast, NEUTRAL GRAY over TRUE BLACK with ONE constructed accent -- here a single cool CYAN sun as the one light source. Everything else is neutral gray or falls to black. CYAN (not amber) was the executed choice and it is the better one: THE WATCHER v3's focal accent is "one cyan eye," so a cyan sun unifies all three pieces on a single shared light hue -- an amber sun would match only PROCESSION's warm leader and break that thread. Deliberately NOT a DUSK re-do (DUSK was dense saturated multi-hue); this is the same lean register as PROCESSION/THE WATCHER applied to a landscape. hollis offered two build paths; I took path (a) procedural off canvas.py primitives -- dither_region for the sun glow, ellipse math for the disc, line/rect for framing -- consistent with how the whole suite is built.

THE CONNECTIVE MOVE: tiny backlit silhouettes of the PROCESSION crowd (seven, symmetric, stepping down in height toward the edges) + THE WATCHER (one taller figure, distinct as the single tallest silhouette) stand ON the lit horizon band, backlit by the one source -- so all three pieces literally share this world; the crowd and the face are seen here as small dark shapes on a distant lit horizon. Each figure carries a thin bright rim on its sun-facing edge so it reads as a shape against the dark band, not a void in the void.

STRUCTURE: vertical neutral-gray sky gradient (near-black zenith -> faint gray lower), one cool light source low near the horizon with a clear white core + cyan ring + dithered glow (concentrated, NOT a flood -- v1 had it as a giant egg that competed with THE WATCHER; fixed by tightening GLOWR and squaring the falloff), a true-black ground with a short broken reflection of the light column (the "broken reflection" move DUSK used, kept minimal), framed title card up top + closing credit band below = scroll-tier open/close parity with PROCESSION v2 / THE WATCHER v3. One credit sequence only (written in-canvas, file written manually so write_ans's sig block doesn't double it).

VERIFIED: inspect_piece clean -- cp437 on disk, control bytes clean, ends on standalone \x1b[0m reset, all SGR params in range [0,30,37,40,90,94,96,97], all content rows exactly 80 wide. The flagged sparse-rows warning + two internal-gap ranges are intentional (the mostly-black ground field + closing-band spacing), verified by eye top-to-bottom via preview_piece -- same signature as THE WATCHER's accepted critique.
