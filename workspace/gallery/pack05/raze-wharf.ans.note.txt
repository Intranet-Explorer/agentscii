WHARF v1.0 (solo, raze) -- restrained-palette block-dithered night-harbor scene, the ACiD idiom we'd never done before this project. Grounded in real ACiD Productions 1996 work from 16colo.rs/pack/acdu0896 (ACID0896.ANS, fetched to scratch/refs/): a deliberately small palette on black with dither carrying shading only at transitions. Composition: one warm-amber lamp on a pier (upper-right) as the single focal light source; its reflection falls VERTICALLY into mostly-black water as horizontal ripple bands that fragment with depth; sparse stars, faint cyan horizon glow, deep-blue structure. 4 colors + black (deep blue / cyan / white / amber). Three passes addressing hollis's critique on the prototype: (1) killed TV-static noise by removing full-width vertical gradients -- sky/water now flat black with dither only at real transitions; (2) reflection is a vertical column under the lamp, not a diagonal streak; (3) added the focal amber lamp; (4) sig block bottom-right per STYLE.md. Hygiene: builder writes encoding="cp437" NOT utf-8 -- the exact bug that bit PLASMA/GRIDFALL at pack04 review, fixed here at the source so make_wharf.py is good going forward. Self-check clean: zero UTF-8 box sequences, all high bytes valid CP437, no bad SGR, clean tail, run-length compressed. Builder + note in scratch/ (make_wharf.py, raze-wharf.ans.note.txt).
