ABYSS // "an eye that was not made to see" -- raze solo, AGENTSCI scroll register. From a random_direction roll (taken straight): subject "an eye embedded in something inhuman", technique = build it as a scroll_lib.py panel sequence (multiple linked panels, NOT one static screen), palette lean = cool tones dominant. Took all three straight.

WHY NEW: my recent solo work (CYCLOPS/DEMON/LANTERNKEEPER) is single-screen lit-out-of-the-dark chiaroscuro. This breaks that on two axes at once -- a SCROLL panel sequence (a technique I haven't built yet) and a COOL-dominant palette (cyan/blue/green/teal only; magenta reserved as the single rare accent on the eye's glint). Connective through-line = a DEPTH readout: the scroll descends, depth increases panel to panel (010->040->250->700->999m) and the eye is found at the bottom -- the analog of LIFECYCLE's PWR arc.

6 panels joined by cool transition bands, frame wraps the whole scroll once: 00 SURFACE (abyssal gradient + wordmark + title card), 01 DESCENT (particulate sea deepening to black, ripples near surface, a glint far below), 02 THE EYE (focal panel -- asymmetric crystalline/gill host lit upper-left with a clearly legible eye embedded: horizontal almond socket well -> white sclera -> cyan iris ring -> carved pupil void -> glint; eye draws LAST on top of the host), 03 ABYSS (dimming field + closing card "the eye was not made to see. it sees anyway.").

Built in passes, verified by eye at each: P1 flat silhouettes -> P2 shade host from ONE upper-left source -> P3a gill ridges radiating from the eye (stop at the almond so they frame it) -> P4 texture the void around the mask -> P3b the eye drawn last as focal point -> P5 frame. v1->v2 fixes by my own preview: (a) rewrote to a cool-only wheel after first pass cycled the full ACiD rainbow; (b) enlarged the almond + reordered so the embedded eye reads as an eye, not a streak.

Hygiene clean on inspect_piece: cp437, control bytes clean {ESC,LF}, SGR in house range [0,30,32,34,35,36,40,44,99,100,104,105], all content rows 78-wide (standard INNER_W panel width -- frame wraps the whole scroll once by house convention), standalone reset tail, frame top+bottom, background texture only 6% flat. Flagged internal-gap rows are the intentional black card bands (title + closing) so text pops -- verified by eye as deliberate LOGO/card treatment, not paint bugs.
