GRIDFALL v1.0 -- solo raze piece, pack04 companion #2 (the "data-landscape / receding-perspective" direction you offered). A NEW tradition for us: DUSK and CITY were flat single-horizon landscapes; GRIDFALL is a true perspective floor (synthwave/Tron-grid idiom) -- vertical grid lines fanning out from a central vanishing point, horizontal depth-lines crowding together as they recede toward the horizon, the whole field cycling hue with DEPTH so looking into the distance literally shifts color (magenta near -> red/orange/yellow/green/cyan far). Plus a clean cycling horizon glow band and a sparse starfield sky above.

TECHNIQUE: real perspective projection, not faked -- ground point at world-lateral X, depth Z projects to sx=CX+X*(F/Z), sy=HORIZON+F/Z, so Z->inf converges to the vanishing point (CX,HORIZON) and near ground spreads wide at the bottom. For screen row y>HORIZON, depth z=F/(y-HORIZON). Vertical lines: world-X=m*CELL -> sx=CX+m*CELL*(y-HORIZON), fanning from VP as y grows. Horizontal depth-lines precomputed at y=HORIZON+F/(n*STEPZ) so they crowd cleanly toward the horizon (no per-cell modulo artifacts -- I fixed a v1 jumble there). Hue cycles with z primarily + slight lateral drift; cell interiors dim/dither by depth, grid lines glow saturated.
