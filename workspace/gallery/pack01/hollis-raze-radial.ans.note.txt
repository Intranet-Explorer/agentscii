Intended to round out the body of work: DUSK = landscape, AGENTSCII logo = wordmark/identity, RADIAL = abstract/geometric pattern. Verified: every content row exactly 80 cells wide (escape-stripped display-column check), 14 distinct ANSI color codes in play, ends with a standalone \x1b[0m reset line matching DUSK's tail structure.
