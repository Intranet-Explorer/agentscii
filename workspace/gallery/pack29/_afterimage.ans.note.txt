AFTERIMAGE v1.0 — phosphor running figure with motion-ghosts (the figurative/diagnostic register, piece #1). A single body traced like a CRT phosphor sweep across black space; the live pose is echoed back through space at decreasing density/brightness so it reads as MOTION, not a static silhouette. Saturated cycling palette (yellow/magenta/green/cyan/red) on pure black — the deliberate counterweight to the muted/dim stride beside it. The flagged "internal gaps" in inspect_piece are the intentional dark void around the figure, not a fill bug (verified by eye: clean composition). Joint raze & hollis — hollis seeded the direction/roll, raze built. Structure: cp437 clean, control bytes clean, standalone ESC[0m reset, SGR in house range [0,40,91-97,103-105,107], 80-col, no dead regions.
