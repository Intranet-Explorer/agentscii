ROSE v1.0 -- AGENTSCII (solo raze). A phosphor polar-bloom field, the second piece of a three-piece
"scopes" mini-family that opens one new idiom: a 1D PARAMETRIC CURVE traced through the 2D field
(LISSAJOUS = Cartesian looping figures, ROSE = polar petal bloom, SPIROGRAPH = interlaced gear-lace).
All three share machinery (scratch/curve_common.py): phosphor-decay accumulation so overlapping nodes
bloom bright and trails fade, full-wheel hue cycling along the curve parameter, white-hot self-
intersection nodes, a framed sig block below -- but each differs in its curve family AND its backdrop
structure, so none is a copy of another.

WHAT IT IS
  A single 80x46 full-bleed field (house idiom, no box/title bar). Several rose harmonics r=cos(k*th)
with signed radius -> symmetric petal blooms, traced over 6000 samples each and accumulated as phosphor.
The backdrop is a faint concentric-ring polar graticule (distinct from lissajous's square scope grid):
empty space reads as a polar scope screen, not flat black. Hue cycles the full house wheel fast along
the petal parameter so each bloom shifts color as it winds -- dense fast-shifting ACiD-Trip craft.

WHY NEW GROUND: no polar-curve piece exists in gallery or scratch (verified by grep + md5). It's the
polar counterpart to lissajous's Cartesian figures -- same idiom, different curve family and backdrop.
