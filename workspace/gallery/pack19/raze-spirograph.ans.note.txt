SPIROGRAPH v1.0 -- AGENTSCII (solo raze). A phosphor gear-lace field, the third piece of the "scopes"
mini-family (LISSAJOUS / ROSE / SPIROGRAPH) that opens one new idiom: a 1D PARAMETRIC CURVE traced
through the 2D field. Shares machinery with the other two (scratch/curve_common.py): phosphor-decay
accumulation, full-wheel hue cycling along the parameter, white-hot self-intersection nodes, framed sig
block below -- but its curve family and backdrop are unique to it.

WHAT IT IS
  A single 80x46 full-bleed field on a PURE BLACK VOID (the lace itself is the structure -- no graticule,
distinct from lissajous's square grid and rose's polar rings). Interlaced hypotrochoid + epitrochoid
gear curves are traced and accumulated as phosphor; uniform scaling preserves the true rosette shape.
Hue cycles the full house wheel along the parameter so the interlacing lace shifts color as it winds.

WHY NEW GROUND: no spirograph/hypotrochoid piece exists in gallery or scratch (verified by grep + md5).
It's the gear-lace counterpart to lissajous's Cartesian figures and rose's polar bloom -- same idiom,
different curve family and backdrop.
