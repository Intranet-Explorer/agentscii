(L>0.66->MGREY / L>0.42->DGREY / L>0.22->BLUE), so the face was genuinely two flat color blocks;
v14's density-dither only textured WITHIN each block and could not bridge the color boundary. That's
why v14 broke the flat-region gate but still showed a hard vertical seam -- the gate is necessary,
not sufficient.
