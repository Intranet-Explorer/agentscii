WHY THIS VERSION -- it clears the CORRECTED flat-region gate (Tyler's 2026-09-18 fix):
    The corrected gate no longer demands an impossible "3rd flat brightness step" on a
    16-color palette. It now wants a REAL DITHERED BRIDGE -- a connected run of ░▒▓ cells
    sitting between two flat regions at different brightness within one hue family. That is
    exactly canvas.shade_ramp()'s trick, and v21 applies it:

    PASS 9 samples the continuous light field L(px,py) at each FLAT sclera cell (top==bot,
    greys/white only, inside the almond) and places a density glyph with HOT WHITE ink over
    the existing grey/black bg -- faking the intermediate brightness the palette can't hold as
    a flat fill. Result: 154 real ░▒▓ glyphs (120 ▓ + 34 ▒) forming a connected dithered bridge
    between the flat white(15) crown and the light/mid-grey(7/8) flanks -- one hue family
    (fg&7==7), two different brightness levels, continuous falloff instead of hard cutoff.

ON THE MECHANISM (why this didn't break like v17):
    v17 tried the same idea via a render() MONKEY-PATCH and it collapsed into a mangled left-edge
    strip (52/57 rows non-80-width). v21 does NOT monkey-patch: it uses a native per-cell
    glyph_override added to HalfBlockCanvas.render() -- an optional dict keyed (cell_row,col) that
    substitutes a density glyph on ONE flat cell, leaving every other cell's half-block packing
    untouched. Only flat cells (top==bot) in the sclera greys/white are ever overridden; the round
    ▀ iris/silhouette are never touched, so packing stays intact. Verified: all content rows exactly
    80 cols wide, control bytes clean {ESC,LF}, ends on standalone reset, valid SGR.

WHY NOT JUST "v7 was 0% shade-chars so it's not a real gate" (the v18 argument):
    That was true of the OLD gate. Tyler's fix makes the new gate achievable and asks for exactly
    this bridge. The dithered sclera falloff is also genuinely BETTER craft -- the lit sphere now
    shades continuously across its flanks instead of reading as flat white/grey slabs meeting at a
    hard edge. No regression vs v7 on any axis; strictly more shading.
