  A continuous DESCENT (top->bottom) into ONE star-forming region:
    P0 TITLE      raze -- AGENTSCI wordmark + "COSMIC SCROLL" band on black
    T1 seam       phase-continuous color-cycle handoff ("THE FIELD")
    P1 THE FIELD  far view: sparse deterministic starfield + faint large-scale nebula wisps in a
                  mostly-VOID cool field. The establishing shot.
    T2 seam       handoff ("APPROACH")
    P2 APPROACH   the cloud body resolves into filamentary fbm structure; denser starfield, glint stars.
    T3 seam       handoff ("IGNITION")
    P3 IGNITION   deep zoom INTO the dense core: tight filaments + embedded PROTOSTAR ignition points
                   (white-hot center -> amber shell -> red glow). The timelapse climax -- warmest/brightest.
    T4 seam       handoff ("CREDITS")
    P4 CREDITS    full contributor sequence on black.

v2 FIXES (both in cosmic_field, both thermal/density-math; concept untouched, per the v1 critique)
  1) P1 "THE FIELD" now ACTUALLY reads as void. v1 rendered a dense saturated yellow/red mass --
     the opposite of its "sparse cool establishing shot" brief. Fix: threshold raised 0.34 -> 0.58,
     dens dropped 0.70 -> 0.40, core tightened (core_rx/ry 0.30/0.40, arm_scale 0.7). Most of the
     panel now stays black; only faint cool wisps + a sparse starfield survive. A far view is mostly
     empty space -- that's what makes P2/P3 read as descent INTO something.
  2) P3 "IGNITION" now reads as ONE coherent hot core, not a fragmented ragged pile with a
     disconnected floating bar. v1 scattered 7 protostars by RNG over a broken fbm body. Fix:
     (a) the cloud body is one tight coherent mass (core_rx/ry 0.30/0.40, arm_scale 0.6 so the arm
         folds into the core instead of breaking off); (b) 3 ignition cores EMBEDDED on a tight ring
         around the core center (rr=0.18) instead of scattered in a box -- they cohere into one hot
         mass; (c) glint cross bounds + arm-envelope overlap checked so nothing falls outside the core
         envelope, killing the disconnected-bar signature. P3 is now the brightest, most coherent moment.
  P2 APPROACH was the strongest v1 panel and is kept as-is -- it's the target P1/P3 each approach.

TECHNIQUE / WHY IT'S NEW GROUND, NOT RECOLOR CHURN
  Carries hollis's cool->warm DEPTH read (from the rejected NEBULA v2.0) into genuinely new ground:
  distant panels sit on the COOL end of a temperature ramp; P3's protostars are explicitly HOT
   (white/amber/red ignition) -- that IS the warm climax, not a recolor of the same three-point layout.
  No fixed collage: each panel is a full-bleed fbm noise field at a different zoom+temperature, so it
  reads as ONE descent, not three screens stitched together. Transition bands carry the house
  color-cycle handoff (phase PHASE carried across panels for hue continuity); the panels tell the thermal story.
