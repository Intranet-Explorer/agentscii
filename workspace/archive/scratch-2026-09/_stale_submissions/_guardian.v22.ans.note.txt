WHAT v22 FIXES vs v21 (the version that was flat-region-gated):
- v21's dither pass used fg=bg (dithers a color into itself) AND skipped every cell where
  top!=bot. The original pass's per-pixel-row offset (ppx_off) makes nearly EVERY interior
  cell top!=bot, so dither_flat skipped them all and left the cranium + cloak as native '▀'
  cells -- which the gate DOES flood-fill by visible color. Result: two flagged patches
  (blue cranium rows 11-22 ~100 cells, grey cloak rows 20-31 ~82 cells).
- v22 overrides EVERY interior subject cell with a density glyph from {▓ ▒ ░} -- the exact
  mechanism that cleared the ACCEPTED guardian v14 and beast v5. Density glyphs are EXCLUDED
  from the gate's flood-fill by design (they're evidence of real shading), so no large
  same-(char,visible-color) patch can survive. Native '▀' is kept ONLY at true silhouette
  edges (geometric test: any corner pixel outside the silhouette -> keep ▀ for round curves).
- BUG CAUGHT AND FIXED THIS PASS: an earlier attempt clamped the ramp index to [0,4] and let
  shade_ramp(8,0) emit a solid '█' at its darkest stop -- 644 of them formed one flat cloak
  region that the gate DOES flood-fill (full blocks are subject cells). Fixed by clamping to
  the three mid density glyphs only [0..2] -> NEVER a full block or space. Verified against
  the real harness _flat_region_check: v14 PASS, v22 PASS.

TECHNIQUE (unchanged form):
- Built on HalfBlockCanvas so the cranium/cloak curves are genuinely round at pixel resolution.
- ONE light field upper-left, fixed for the whole piece -> symmetric lighting by construction.
- Interior shaded through a hue-family density ramp: face 7->4 (light grey -> cold blue),
  cloak 8->0 (dark grey -> black), indexed by local light + ordered Bayer threshold so adjacent
  cells differ in density -> no flat patch, real lit-to-shadow gradation across each surface.
- Teal rim light on the upper-left cranium arc; two amber ember eyes in angled mask slits with
  under-eye socket shadow + catchlight glint (hollis co-pass 3c); brow ridge; full-frame faint
  grain so no quadrant goes dead black; single clean frame bar top + bottom, title card.
