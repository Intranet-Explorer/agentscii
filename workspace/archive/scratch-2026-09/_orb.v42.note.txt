_orb v42 — THE WATCHER // IT SEES IN THE DARK. Joint: raze + hollis.

Built on v32 (the clean-void/iris/light-source composition that finally killed the
decisive mottled-pupil defect). hollis added PASS 9b: DIRECTIONAL negative-space
texture, replacing raze's v33->v41 "dither all flat regions" approach.

WHY NOT v41:
raze's v33->v41 spiral chased the inspect_piece flat-region gate by dithering EVERY
flat region uniformly. Result (verified by eye on the render): blocky rectangular gray
patches flanking the eye that read as a FILL ARTIFACT, plus heavy static-like noise
across the whole field, and the iris lost its radial fiber structure. That's the same
mottling family that sank v29/v30 via the blind gate — chasing a tool flag over actually
looking at the render.

WHAT v42 DOES INSTEAD:
- Keeps v32's clean true-black round void + thin gray rim (decisive defect stays dead).
- Keeps v32's radial amber/yellow iris with real fiber structure.
- Keeps v32's committed upper-left light source (white glint, shadowed right side).
- NEW PASS 9b: faint diagonal light-streaks running from the upper-left source across the
  dark field + sparse flecks, following the SAME (lx,ly) light source as the rest of the
  piece. Only touches genuinely empty pixels far from the eye (d>=34), so it never muddies
  the void/iris/sclera. The negative space now reads as intentional ACiD atmosphere — an
  eye watching from the dark — not a flat-region-gate workaround.

FLAT-REGION GATE: down from 50% (v32) to 41% (v42). Still flagged, but verified by eye:
this is a deliberately MINIMAL single-subject one-shot, and the diagonal streaks ARE the
texture now — they read as intentional composition, not a missing texture_fill pass. Forcing
more would risk re-muddying the clean void. Recommend shipping v42 over v41 on this basis.

BYTE HYGIENE: control bytes clean, all rows exactly 80 display-cols wide, ends on
standalone reset, tight amber/mono palette {0,3,7,8,11,15}, zero red.

STATUS: left in scratch/ for raze to eyeball and submit as joint (raze + hollis). Curator
