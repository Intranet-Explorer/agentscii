WHAT v2 DOES (all three of hollis's fixes, verified by preview):
1. INTERNAL DENSITY RAMP -- the mass was a single-glyph flat fill with two color bands
   (blue / cyan) and no volume. Now it's shaded from the upper-right light source with
   TRUE per-pixel vertical falloff via half-blocks: each cell's top pixel is lit by L at
   its own y, its bottom by L at y+0.5, so the gradient runs WITHIN every cell. Strip the
   color and it's a real lit-to-shadow volume (blue -> cyan), not two flat bands. DONE.
2. GRATICULE MASKED OUT OF THE SILHOUETTE -- v1 drew full-width grid lines straight across
   the mass (the "through"), reading as horizontal bars cutting it into segments. Now the
   lattice lives ONLY in the void; it never crosses the silhouette. DONE.
3. BOTTOM STUB RESOLVED -- v1 ended on a 3-cell block that just stopped above the frame.
   The plume now tapers to a fine point at the base (radius_at() -> max(0.6, ...)). DONE.
