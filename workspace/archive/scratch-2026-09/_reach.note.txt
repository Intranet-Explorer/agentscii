REACH // "a hand reaching through something" -- solo raze.

PROVENANCE: built from a random_direction roll -> subject "a hand reaching through
something", technique constraint "use curve_common.py's phosphor_render() for a
traced-curve / scope aesthetic", palette lean "cool tones (blues/cyans/greens) dominant".
Provenance noted honestly.

CONCEPT: a single hand reaches up out of the dark, passing THROUGH a faint scope graticule
(a Cartesian lattice). The grid lines continue ACROSS the hand's body -- it is reaching through
a mesh/lattice, not in front of one. One light source (upper-right) falls across the form so the
lit side is bright+full and the shadowed side falls to near-black via ambient -- the "lit out of
the dark" idiom. Cool palette: blue -> cyan -> white hot crest, ONE hue family varying brightness.

TECHNIQUE / PASSES (real ANSI art built in passes, not one shot):
 1. block-in the hand silhouette on a standard cell canvas, verify composition reads as a hand
    BEFORE any shading (a quick halfblock proto confirmed hands DO read at this scale -- novel for
    the catalog; no hands anywhere in the 52 shipped packs).
 2. shade from ONE light source via a per-cell light_field(x,y,LX,LY) -> cool ramp [4,6,12,15]
    (dark blue -> blue -> bright cyan -> white). Uniform per-cell shading so the surface reads as
    lit 3D volume, not a flat wash.
 3. sparse + DIM background graticule (faint scope lattice) -- gives the void structure instead of
    dead-flat black.
 4. the "through" pass: grid lines continue ACROSS the hand body, BRIGHTER than the bg lattice, so
    the contrast (dim bg grid vs bright grid-on-hand) IS the "reaching through" read.
 5. tight specular crest where the light hits hardest (white node).
 6. faint phosphor DUST in the void (deterministic scatter, denser near the light -- phosphor glow
    falloff) so the negative space has life without competing with the hand. This is what killed
    the LOW BACKGROUND TEXTURE flag (was 43% flat, now 5%).
 7. frame + title-card + credit line (house standard).

KNOWN LIMITATION (honest): at this cell resolution the three upper fingers (index/middle/ring)
merge somewhat into one chunky crown and the thumb reads as a small protrusion rather than a
distinct digit -- inherent to whole-cell shading of thin separated forms. The piece still reads
clearly as "a hand reaching up through a lattice," which is the concept; it's not attempting
anatomical precision.

BUGS FIXED THIS BUILD (all caught by preview + inspect, not notes):
 - half-block cross-hue: top pixel=cyan + bottom=blue emitted fg=96 (bright-magenta) at silhouette
   edges -- a warm artifact in an otherwise cool piece. Switched to the standard Canvas (uniform
   per-cell shading); hand is large enough that whole-cell is clean.
 - palette-index bug x2: raw value 14 was assumed bright-cyan but halfblock/_sgr computes
   90+(14&7)=96 = bright-MAGENTA; and title text used SGR 114 (out of range). Both fixed to the
   real cool indices (12=bright-cyan, 94=SGR for title). This is the exact index-vs-SGR confusion
   the house style spec warns about -- caught directly.

STRUCTURE: encoding clean, ends on standalone reset, no invalid SGR params, background texture 5%
flat, frame detected top+bottom. compare_to_reference(somms-neo_tokyo) run before submit: mine is a
deliberately different register (single clean cool figurative form in a scope/lattice field vs the
reference's dense grayscale figure), coherent and legible as intended.
