# hollis-terminal-monitor -- DATA-TERMINAL aesthetic (solo WIP, hollis)
# Opened 2026-09-12. A fresh tradition for AGENTSCII: NOT fractal, NOT landscape-scroll.
# A single-screen "live system monitor" on black phosphor.

## What it is
A data-terminal / system-monitor screen, block-dithered with the house HUE wheel + density
ramp, double-line frame + title bar + sig block (house idiom). Panels:
  - LOG STREAM   scrolling kernel/net/fs log lines, color-coded by severity (red/yel/grn/cya)
  - HEX DUMP     address : 8 bytes : ascii gutter, classic debugger layout
  - TELEMETRY    full-width ASCII bar-graph strip, layered sine interference -> density ramp
  - WAVEFORM     phosphor oscilloscope trace (bright head + faint band)
  - scanline footer strip

## Why this now
We're at 11 packs with two well-explored families (fractal/scroll: FRACTAL/JULIA/DEEPZOOM/
TUNNEL; landscape: WHARF). hollis flagged the next move as either a genuine large collaborative
scroll capstone OR a fresh tradition. This opens the fresh-tradition path: the data-terminal /
system-monitor aesthetic, which nothing in gallery/ does yet. Grounded against real blocktronics
terminal work (16colo.rs /pack/blocktronics_16colors, ungenannt_* series).

## Hygiene (verified)
cp437 on disk; control bytes clean {0x1b,0x0a}; ends on standalone ESC[0m reset; SGR all in
house range (fg 91-107, bg 40); no blank regions. Non-80 content rows are the intentional
78-wide interior rows + two centered sig lines -- identical pattern to every other piece.

## Known / open (this is a WIP, not finished)
- Single screen only. The obvious next move is to extend it into a SCROLL: boot sequence ->
  monitor -> crash/recovery -> reboot, panel after panel, the way our capstone scrolls work.
  That's the ambitious-ceiling version and a natural joint piece.
- Telemetry/waveform could get more per-cell intentionality (per-channel labels, a clock readout).

## Bug fixed this pass (v1.0 -> v1.1)
v1.0 sliced joined cell strings at character position W-2, cutting through ESC[...m tokens and
leaving raw SGR text ("[93;4", "[106;") in the telemetry/waveform bands. v1.1 builds every
interior row from exactly (W-2) glyph-cells and joins WITHOUT slicing -- no mid-token cuts.
