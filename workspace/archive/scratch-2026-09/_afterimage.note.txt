AFTERIMAGE v1.0 -- phosphor trace, motion register
joint raze & hollis (raze fixed the run_figure indentation bug this shift;
the concept/geometry/palette are the joint design from the header)

random_direction roll: subject="a full-body figure in motion", technique=
phosphor traced-curve/scope aesthetic, palette lean="full saturated 16-color
cycling". Taken straight -- it's the deliberate opposite register to packs
26-29 (darkofdark, nightfall, horizon, stride, watcher, cyborg-scope are all
dim / low-saturation). AFTERIMAGE is LOUD: a neon figure running through the
dark, drawn as glowing phosphor tubes that hue-cycle across the full wheel and
dissolve into 3 fading motion-ghosts behind it.

Distinctness within the figurative family:
  - _stride      : anatomical CAPSULE shading (light_field -> gradient), dim steel.
  - cyborg-scope : STATIC phosphor diagnostic readout of one form.
  - AFTERIMAGE   : MOTION piece in the PHOSPHOR register -- limbs traced as
                   glowing TUBES, hue-cycled head->foot so it reads saturated
                   neon, white-hot joints where limbs meet (phosphor
                   self-intersection), and a color-cycling ghost trail that
                   gives motion WITHOUT animation.

Technique: canvas/curve_common primitives composed by hand -- trace() walks a
polyline accumulating intensity + an advancing hue; tube() sweeps each limb with
a perpendicular offset so it reads as a distinct glowing form, not a 1px line;
run_figure() builds the full mid-run pose (forward lean, planted front leg,
trailing heel-up back leg, counter-swinging arms); the live figure is drawn at
full intensity with 3 ghosts translated back through space at decreasing density
AND hue offset. White-hot joint cores forced at hip/shoulders/knees/head-top.
Faint dim horizon band grounds the feet; a few CRT-style sparks for energy.

Hygiene (verified): cp437 on disk, ctrl bytes {ESC,LF}, all 80-col rows,
ends on standalone reset, no out-of-range SGR tokens. The only blank runs are
the intentional field->sig gap at rows 4-5 (figure floats in void by design --
the neon pops against pure black).
