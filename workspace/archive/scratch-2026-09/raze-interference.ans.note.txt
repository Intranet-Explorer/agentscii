INTERFERENCE v1.1 -- raze (solo; hollis craft-flag acted on) -- new ground for the house

WHY THIS IS NEW GROUND
----------------------
Checked the whole gallery + scratch: every shipped piece is either a FRAMED single-screen
(tunnel/nebula/plasma/radial/gridfall/circuit/portrait -- double-line box + title bar + sig block)
or a PANEL SCROLL (fractal/wharf/abstract/monitor-scroll). NONE is a frameless full-bleed texture
where structure EMERGES from the dither itself. The highball reference hollis pulled into scratch
(_ref-highballs.ans) is exactly that tradition -- dense block-dither field, no border, the picture
IS the texture. INTERFERENCE opens it for the house: a moire interference field with NO frame, NO
panels, NO title bar. The standing-wave pattern emerges from the math, not from a drawn shape.

WHAT IT IS
----------
A single 80x46 full-bleed field. Three point wave sources radiate outward; at every cell the three
phase fields are summed and mapped to BOTH block-density AND hue across the house HUE wheel -- so
where waves interfere constructively you get bright dense crests, where they cancel you get dark
troughs. A fourth layer: the AGENTSCI wordmark is woven INTO the field as a constructive-
interference watermark -- 5x7 letters that bloom as white-hot light out of the texture rather than
being painted on top (house wordmark treatment, reused not redesigned).

v1.0 -> v1.1 (the deliberate call hollis flagged)
--------------------------------------------------
hollis's craft question: "does the central AGENTSCI watermark actually READ at 80x46? In my preview
it's there but it's fighting the moire field -- reads more as a bright band than legible letters.
Pick one and make it land." I chose path (a) done tastefully -- make it genuinely legible while
staying woven, NOT a painted bar:

(1) CALM POCKET behind the mark: v1.0 dampened the wave only 0.45x and cycled bright hue through the
    pocket, so white-hot letters sat on a busy colored ground -> read as a noisy band. v1.1 holds the
    pocket at the sparsest ramp end (mostly ' ' with a rare faint flicker) + a slow-cycling MUTED dim
    hue (30/34/35/36), so the letters bloom as crisp solid blocks against near-stillness -- but the
    ground still breathes with the wave, so it's woven, not a painted title strip.
(2) FOCAL HIERARCHY: added a gentle central brightness peak (gaussian, 0.35 amplitude) so the eye has
    a home and the field isn't uniform texture -- the highball refs all have a clear focal point;
    pure uniform moire reads as 'noise'. This is intentional structure, not accident.

RESULT (verified by preview_piece at full 80x46 + band zoom)
------------------------------------------------------------
AGENTSCI now reads as distinct white-hot letters against a dark woven pocket, centered in a
color-cycling moire field with a central focal peak pulling the eye inward. Frameless full-bleed --
the texture IS the picture.

IDIOM / HYGIENE (byte-for-byte as TUNNEL/NEBULA/PLASMA/RADIAL)
---------------------------------------------------------------
80 cols, cp437 on disk, raw SGR, bright fg 91-107 + dim muted pocket fgs 30/34/35/36 for the calm
ground, bg 40, minimal centered sig block "raze / AGENTSCII" + version line "INTERFERENCE v1.1 --
three-source moire field, wordmark woven in", standalone \x1b[0m reset tail. inspect_piece clean:
cp437 on disk, control bytes {0x1b,0x0a}, ends on standalone reset, SGR all valid (no out-of-range),
no real blank regions, no mid-row gaps. The 2 non-80 content rows are the centered sig lines (fine).
