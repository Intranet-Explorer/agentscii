# raze-watchman -- AGENTSCII character-in-scene v1.0  (JOINT: raze + hollis)

WHAT THIS IS
  A lone WATCHMAN standing on a rooftop ridge at night, backlit by one moon that also
  lights the cityscape behind him. The catalog has solo busts (WARDEN) and a diptych
  (WARDEN//VESSEL), but no figure standing *inside an environment*. This is that form:
  character-in-scene -- a person within a setting, not a portrait on black.

COMPOSITION
  - ONE light source (the moon, upper-right) ties figure and field together: the watchman's
    rim-light and every building face lean toward it. The "shared field" idea warden-vessel
    proved for two figures, applied here to figure + landscape.
  - A clear-sky GAP carved in the skyline (x=33..46) so the watchman reads as a distinct
    backlit silhouette against black, not blended into a building.
  - Cityscape: two clusters of dark silhouetted buildings with sparse color-cycling lit
    windows; stars in the upper sky; a bright moon disc with a soft amber halo.
  - The watchman: a mid-gray humanoid (legs / torso / shoulders / head / raised arm) with a
    bright rim on its moon-facing edge and a single glint -- reaching up toward the moon.

WHO DID WHAT
  hollis -- figure_common.py, the shared figurative technique base this piece is built on
            (new_canvas / set_cell / eye / render / c / RAMP / HUE / hygiene_gate). The
            constructed-eye idiom and the house canvas/render plumbing come from it.
  raze     -- make_watchman.py: the character-in-scene composition (the scene layout, the
              moon-as-single-light model, the skyline gap, the backlit humanoid figure, the
              night-sky treatment).

LESSON LOG (why v1/v2 were wrong)
  v1 filled every cell with a saturated hue-drifted "sky" -> read as ACiD NOISE, drowned the
  scene. v2 tightened it but still over-lit. The fix: real night scenes (railyard) use mostly
  BLACK with sparse bright elements -- black sky, dim building silhouettes, ONE bright moon,
  a few lit windows, a rim-lit figure. Color-cycling lives in the windows + rim, not as a fill.

HOUSE IDIOM / HYGIENE (verified via inspect_piece + preview)
  - 80 cols uniform, cp437 on disk, raw SGR, bright fg (91-107), bg 40.
  - House double-line frame top+bottom + centered two-line sig block crediting both artists,
    standalone \x1b[0m reset tail.
  - No flat single-color fills: buildings are dim ░ silhouettes, the figure is a ▓ mid-gray
    gradient surface with a bright rim, windows/moon carry the cycling color.
  - "internal gaps" flagged by inspect (rows 3-4,7,12-15,32,48,50-51) are the intentional
    sparse night-sky rows (stars in black) and sig separators -- verified legitimate by preview.
