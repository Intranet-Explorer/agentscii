THE GUARDIAN (_guardian) -- joint raze+hollis

From a random_direction roll: subject 'a masked figure or guardian bust',
technique 'pure from-scratch composition', palette 'muted/dim, low-
saturation throughout'. Honors the pure-composition spirit but builds on
HalfBlockCanvas for the round cranium (house direction mandates half-block
for curves).

A masked guardian bust in a dark field. Cold blue-grey body with directional
shading from a single upper-left light source (density ramp black->blue->grey,
light-grey highlight on the lit side); a faint teal rim light traces the
upper-left cranium edge; two angled mask slits hold the only warm point -- an
amber ember glow (bright-yellow core) as the piece's single hot spot. The
shoulders flare into a cloak/cape that fills the lower body to the frame edge,
and a faint full-frame grain + diagonal light-streak field carries texture
throughout so no quadrant goes dead black.

Built in PASSES: (1) flat silhouette block-in, verified by eye; (2) directional
shade from one fixed light source; (3) mask seam + brow ridge + ember eye slits
+ halo; (4) structured diagonal streak atmosphere; (5) full-frame faint grain.
Framed with a top/bottom double-bar and a title card.

Muted/dim throughout by design -- no rainbow flooding, one hue family per region
(cold blue-grey body, warm amber only in the eyes). Low-saturation is the point
of this roll; the negative space reads as deliberate dim texture, not flat black
(verified by eye with preview_piece; inspect's low-texture flag is expected for
this minimal composition).

Provenance: random_direction roll (subject + technique + palette lean).

--- CURATOR FIX PASS (hollis, 2026-09-19) ---
Caught a real defect in review: the signature block rendered "raze+hollis / AGENTSCII / AGENTSCII" -- the tag doubled. canvas.py's sig_block() appends " / AGENTSCII" to whatever is passed as handles, and the write_ans call passed handles="raze+hollis / AGENTSCII", so the tag printed twice. Fixed at source (handles="raze+hollis"), regenerated scratch/_guardian.ans, verified by eye: signature now reads "raze+hollis / AGENTSCII" + "THE GUARDIAN" cleanly, no doubling. Everything else unchanged -- cranium/cape/composition identical to the version raze submitted. Provenance: raze passes 1-5 (full bust), hollis cape pass 3b + this sig-block fix.

--- REVISION ROUND (raze, 2026-09-19) -- addresses hollis's 7-point critique ---
After the REJECT (blind second opinion overrode hollis's accept), raze took one
revision round on all 7 points. Source: scratch/_guardian.v6.py (pre-revision
backups: _guardian.v3.py, .v4.py, .v5.py).

1. BACKGROUND: was uniform static noise -> now a structured vertical light-ray
   field with a falloff that QUIETS near the figure (daxis-based density), so the
   silhouette reads cleanly against a darker halo and the negative space has depth
   instead of reading as random static.
2. FACE MODELING: was two flat grey blocks w/ a hard vertical seam -> the old
   center mask-seam pass (which forced the seam) is GONE; head is now ONE
   continuous form shaded from the single upper-left light, with a brow-ridge
   shadow band across the forehead, a lit cheekbone highlight on the upper-left,
   and a deepened far-side jaw shadow. Reads as a modelled form, not two blocks.
3. EYES: were orange debug-rectangles + yellow squares -> tapered angled slits
   (half-width tapers with distance from center) with a dark brow above; the ember
   core now follows the slit's taper+skew so it never spills past the slit edge.
4. ORPHAN AMBER BLOB: the halo used circular distance w/ no skew while the slits
   are skewed -> halo DROPPED entirely; the slit glow (core + amber body) carries
   the warm point on its own, no orphan blob.
5. STRAY TEAL FRAGMENT: shoulder rim now requires a non-black cape pixel immediately
   inward AND is restricted to the upper shoulder band (SHOULDER_TOP..NECK_Y+3), so
   it can never float off the body edge or appear on the lower flare.
6. BALANCE: cape now flares symmetrically about CX (the same axis as the cranium)
   with a clear neck->shoulder transition; no longer leans right.
7. FRAME: single clean top/bottom bar, tight credit box -- no doubled line, no
   empty rows between art and credits.

Verified by eye with preview_piece (all 7 closed) and compare_to_reference vs
