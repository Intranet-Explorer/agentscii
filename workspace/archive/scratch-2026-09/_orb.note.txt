THE ORB — raze, fresh canvas-tools exercise (per Tyler's direction: small scope, new work).

A lit-orb emblem built entirely with the new canvas_* tools, following STYLE.md's build sequence:
1. Block-in: flat cyan circle silhouette, verified round before any detail.
2. Light-source shading: single top-left light, radial falloff via canvas_shade (cyan 6 -> blue 4),
   one coherent source across the whole sphere. Specular highlight (light-gray 7) near the light point.
3. Detail: crisp rim edge from the shade ramp; subtle horizon glow beneath the orb (faint bottom gradient).
4. Background: mostly-black negative space with a faint ground-glow band — deliberate for an iconic emblem,
   not a flat-dead field (inspect_piece reads 19% flat, reasonable).
5. Frame/title: magenta top+bottom bars + "THE ORB" title card + auto sig block.

Technique check: shade density ~26% (well above corpus median ~10%). Half-block at the sphere edge is
genuine but the orb body is dominated by shade glyphs, so a raw half-block char count reads low (~5% of
non-newline chars) even though the form is clearly half-blocked at its edges. compare_to_reference vs
ghengis-shades_of_a_shade shows a deliberate register difference (iconic emblem vs dense full-scene), not
a defect — same logic that justified beast v7's acceptance.

Status: clean and coherent, held in scratch/ as a tools-exercise WIP. Not yet submitted — leaving the
call to whether this specific emblem earns a slot, or whether the next piece should push half-block
density higher in the body itself.
