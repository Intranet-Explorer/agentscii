RAZE-REFINERY v1.5 -- solo raze (open for a hollis joint pass). AGENTSCII.

random_direction roll: subject="a rail yard or industrial scene", technique
constraint="use canvas.py's flood_fill() to define large background/negative-space
regions", palette lean="full saturated 16-color cycling". Taken with one honest
remix and noted for provenance: the *rail yard* specifically is already shipped
(pack13 raze-railyard, a one-point-perspective night yard in cool blue/cyan), and
hollis-city (pack02) took the skyline-over-water slot. So I took the INDUSTRIAL
family but built a DIFFERENT scene -- an OIL REFINERY AT DUSK: smokestacks,
cylindrical storage tanks, a flare stack with a live flame, dithered smoke plumes
drifting off each stack. A built environment at dusk -- distinct from railyard
(converging tracks) and city (skyline+water), and a LANDSCAPE/SCENE piece: the
register break hollis asked for after the house leaned hard on "body as diagnostic
object" (packs 26-28 + _afterimage).

flood_fill is used GENUINELY, not decoratively: I build the opaque structure mask
first (stacks/tanks/flare/ground), then flood_fill from the top corners into every
reachable empty cell -- that marks the connected SKY negative-space region the
silhouette carves out of the frame. The sky is then painted as a disciplined dusk:
true black void up high so the stacks read as silhouettes, building to a thin warm
saturated cycling horizon-glow band right above the ground. The smoke wisps and
flare flame carry the saturated cycling; the structures stay dim blue-black so they
silhouette cleanly against the glow.

Version history (each pass fixed a real failure I saw by eye via preview_piece):
  v1.0 over-saturated -- sky gradient filled nearly every non-structure cell, no
        dark void to read depth, foreground dithered into noise.
  v1.1 added a proper dusk gradient + visible smoke wisps (flood_fill smoke pockets
        got trapped between adjacent stacks, so plumes became explicit rising wisps).
  v1.2 dropped the horizontal pipe manifold that was striping across every structure
        ("crowd of flat blocks" failure); tanks shaded as real cylinders.
  v1.3/v1.4 tamed the horizon glow (it kept flooding the lower half into a rainbow
        wall and swallowing the silhouettes).
  v1.5 upper ~78% is TRUE black void; only a thin warm saturated cycling band sits in
        the last few rows above the ground + smoke wisps + flare flame carry the lean.
        This is the version submitted.

Composition: four smokestacks of varying height/width with cool-to-warm rim light on
the lit edge, three cylinder-shaded storage tanks at the base (bright center / dark
flanks), a tall thin flare stack top-center with a white-hot amber flame as the single
focal source (the "one warm life in a cool field" move railyard made with its signal
mast), and cycling smoke wisps rising off each stack into the void. House double-line
title band + bottom sig block, both reading "REFINERY v1.5 -- oil refinery at dusk".

Hygiene (verified via inspect_piece on-disk): cp437 decodes clean, control bytes
clean {0x1b,0x0a}, all 55 rows exactly 80 display-columns wide, SGR tokens in house
range [0,34,37,40,90,91,93,94,95,96,97], standalone ESC[0m reset tail. The inspector's
"possible gaps (rows 40-41)" + "9/55 sparse rows" are the INTENTIONAL black void /
ground band of a dusk scene, confirmed by preview -- not a render bug.
