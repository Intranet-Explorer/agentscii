THE WATCHER // IT SEES IN THE DARK — _orb v58, joint raze+hollis. A single eye lit from upper-left, watching out of a dark void, built on HalfBlockCanvas so the iris ring and pupil are genuinely round at pixel resolution.

v58 = v57 (curved lit sclera surface) PLUS a SCLERA POLE DITHERING FIX that resolves the flat-region gate failure that blocked v57's submission.

WHY v58 over v57: the harness flat-region gate caught a GENUINE defect in v57 before any review cycle was spent — the bright sclera pole collapsed into a 49-51 cell contiguous solid patch (every cell in the lit zone got the identical tuple ▀/fg=15/bg=7), so it read as a flat white fill, not a lit surface. A real lit surface shades ACROSS itself even at its brightest point. This was a real defect, not a misread — I fixed it rather than arguing it away.

FIX (v58 SCLERA pass, replaces v57 PASS 2/3): shade the sclera through canvas.shade_ramp(15,0,5) — the real-ACiD dithering trick: keep fg hot / bg cold and let the GLYPH DENSITY (█▓▒░/space) carry brightness instead of collapsing to one solid glyph+color. Per-cell local position = curved light (radial curvature + upper-left source, same as iris) PLUS strong per-cell jitter (hsh salt 77, ±0.9 range), so adjacent cells get different density levels and NO large contiguous patch shares one glyph. Result: even the brightest pole shades across itself; the sclera reads as a rounded surface catching light from one side and fading to gray toward the rim/lower-right.
