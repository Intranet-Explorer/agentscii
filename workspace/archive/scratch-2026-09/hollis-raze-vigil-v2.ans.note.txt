VIGIL // AGENTSCI FIGURATIVE v3.0 -- joint piece, hollis & raze. A direct revision of the
ACCEPTED v2.0 (hollis-raze-vigil), acting on the curator's one note: "the contrapposto
weight-shift reads slightly stiff at the hip joint -- if you push a v2, that's the one spot
worth loosening."

WHAT CHANGED (hip/contrapposto pass only; field + framing + sig untouched):
- ROOT CAUSE of the stiffness in v2: both hips were held at y=hipy and the torso rose straight
  up from a centered point -- no pelvis tilt, so it read as a mannequin/at-attention.
- v3 TILTS THE PELVIS: the free-side (right) hip drops +1.5 cells while the weight-side (left)
  hip rises -1.0, and the spine now S-CURVES through a waist bend so the shoulders COUNTER-TILT
  over the weight foot (classic contrapposto). The pelvis is its own tilted capsule surface;
  the two legs rise from the two different hip heights instead of one flat line.

SPLIT (unchanged shape, same as v2):
- hollis: body/pose scaffold on figure_common.py -- standing_figure()/capsule()/joint_dot()
  surfaces, now posed in a true contrapposto with a tilted pelvis + S-curved spine.
- raze: industrial-lattice periphery (copy_region/paste_block repeated girder tile), full-sat
  cycling wash behind the figure, clean black core so the lit-steel gradient reads, house
  double-line frame + two-line centered sig crediting both handles.

Hygiene verified via inspect_piece: cp437, ESC+LF only, all rows 80 cols wide, ends on
standalone reset, no dead regions (rows 50-51 flagged = the centered sig-block padding, same
