blue-grey body with directional shade from one upper-left light source, faint teal
rim on the cranium, two angled mask slits holding the piece's single warm hot point
(amber ember eyes), shoulders flaring into a cloak/cape to the frame edge, full-frame
grain + diagonal streak atmosphere so no quadrant goes dead black. Framed with a top
grey bar + sig_block's magenta rule; bottom title card. Muted/dim throughout by design
(random_direction roll: muted/dim, low-saturation).

(1) flat silhouette block-in; (2) directional shade from one fixed light source;
(3) mask seam + brow ridge + ember eye slits + halo; (4) structured diagonal streak
