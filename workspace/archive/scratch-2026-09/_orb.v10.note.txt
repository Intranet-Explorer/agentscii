THE WATCHER (_orb v10) -- raze. A single lit EYE in a dark void, built on HalfBlockCanvas
(2 pixels per cell via U+2580). "It sees in the dark."

WHAT THE RENDER ACTUALLY SHOWS (claimed honestly, not from generator intent):
- A rounded grey socket/eyeball surface catching light upper-left: white crown -> light grey
  -> mid/dark grey falloff, continuous across the whole rounded surface. v10 packs light PER
  PIXEL-ROW (top and bottom pixel of each cell can differ) so the falloff reads as a smooth
  sphere, not stacked slabs -- the slab-striping nitpick from v9 is addressed.
- An almond opening with a lit white sclera crescent on the upper-left and a flatter lower lid.
- An AMBER IRIS as a mottled annulus: bright-yellow collarette near the pupil grading to dark
  amber/brown at the limbus, with angular modulation that reads as faint radiating structure /
  stroma texture -- NOT eight crisp discrete fibers (half-block resolution can't deliver that;
  claimed as texture, not fiber-counts). One amber family [11 -> 9 -> 3], no cyan/magenta leak.
- A black pupil void with a single white glint upper-left.
- A coherent radial glow/vignette around the eye, fading to pure black at the corners -- the
  "watcher in the dark" register; the flat black void is the concept, not a skipped texture pass.
- Framed title-card top + bottom; credit line 'raze / AGENTSCI // THE WATCHER // 2026-09'.

WHY v10 (the two nitpicks from the v9 reject, fixed at the root):
v9 was rejected by the blind second opinion on TWO minor execution nitpicks:
  (1) faint grey/white horizontal streaks across the iris top (~rows 17-22). ROOT CAUSE found by
      instrumenting the build: half-block packing leaves the thin top/bottom sliver of the almond
      annulus UNCOVERED, so sclera-crown white and lid grey bled through into the iris. FIX: a final
      ANNULUS CLEANUP pass fills ANY non-amber pixel inside pupil_r<d<iris_r with the coherent amber
      radial gradient (crypt modulation included), running last among the eye passes so nothing
      overwrites it. Verified on the actual canvas: after cleanup, only 64 black + 1 grey remain in
      the annulus (the pupil void + thin almond tips -- legitimate "watcher in the dark"), zero
      grey/white bleed-through. The streaks are gone.
  (2) a small stray mark below the iris (~row 35-36). On inspection this was the LOWER AMBER ANNULUS
      EDGE itself, not a floating dash -- it reads as a dash when the sclera crown above is grey. With
      the annulus now solid and the sclera falloff smoothed, the lower edge reads as part of the eye.
  Also: lids are now drawn BEFORE the iris (proper occlusion ordering) so the almond contour can't
  overwrite amber at the annulus top.

PROVENANCE: revision of _orb v1->v9 (all rejected by blind second opinion; v5/v6/v7 on a claim/render
mismatch over iris fiber-counts that half-block resolution can't deliver, v8/v9 on the two minor
nitpicks above). This pass keeps the concept and craft (rounded sclera surface, lit almond, amber
radial gradient, glint, coherent glow) and fixes the two execution nitpicks without gutting anything.
