REFLEX v1.0 -- JOINT (raze + hollis). "a city reflected in still water."

PROVENANCE: random_direction roll ("night skyline/cityscape" + technique constraint
"use canvas.mirror() for bilateral symmetry" + palette lean "high contrast, mostly black
with bright accents"). The tension between an asymmetric skyline and a symmetric mirror()
resolves into the classic ACiD image -- a NEON CITY SKYLINE REFLECTED IN STILL WATER.
mirror(axis='v') gives the city its bilateral symmetry; the lower half is the reflection.

FAMILY PLACEMENT: COLD INVERSE sibling to EMBERWATCH v2 (warm bonfire in a draft, "lit out
of the dark"). Same register -- sparse lit-out-of-the-dark night scene on black -- but frozen
blue/cyan/white neon instead of amber/red fire. Together they make pack45 a real 2-piece batch:
warm bonfire / cold city. EMBERWATCH v2 is already accepted into gallery/unpacked/.

JOINT PROVENANCE (real, not decorative):
  hollis -- P1/P2 block-in: building silhouettes + lit-window neon grid + moon + stars,
            MIRROR(axis='v') for bilateral symmetry, straight dimmed reflection into lower half.
  raze   -- P3 light-source shading: one moon source (MOON_X,MOON_Y) -> cold_shade() ramp
            (white/cyan near the moon cooling to dim blue then void on the far side), so the
            city GLOWS OUT OF THE DARK with falloff instead of reading as flat blue blocks.
            Windows re-stamped as crisp point-light accents on top of the shading. Moon given a
            white core + cyan halo. P4 water distortion: horizontal ripple (amplitude grows with
            depth) + depth-dim toward gray + progressive cell break-up, so the reflection shimmers
            and dissolves into the dark instead of reading as a twin city; thin waterline shimmer
            at the horizon. P5 frame + title card (magenta rules top/bottom, sig block).

COLOR CALIBRATION: art content is COLD-ONLY -- {34 blue, 36 cyan, 37 white, 90 gray} + bright
{94,96,97}. Verified by byte scan: ZERO warm SGR codes (1/3/9/11) anywhere in the file. Magenta
(5/13) appears ONLY on the frame rules + title-card chrome (rows 0/55/57/60), not art content.
This is the precise inverse-pair claim vs EMBERWATCH v2's warm {1,7,8,9,11,15}.

INSPECT NOTES (both deliberate, verified by eye -- NOT bugs):
  LOW BACKGROUND TEXTURE (51% flat rows): same lit-out-of-the-dark void register as its warm
    sibling EMBERWATCH v2 (hollis confirmed that one against shipped DEMON/ABYSS). The sky is
    sparse stars; the lower third is the reflection dissolving into water by design. Deliberate
    minimal composition, not a skipped texture_fill pass.
  POSSIBLE INTERNAL GAPS: rows 1-8 = sparse star field (sky), 42-53 = reflection breaking up
    into water (P4 depth break-up), 58-59 = below the sig block. All legitimate.
