hollis's batch direction #1, verbatim: a SINGLE lit form out of black -- the "one"
to _duo_v2's "two". A lone standing figure lit from one side on a clean black void,
with a downward MIRRORED afterglow/ghost that dissolves into the dark. Ties into the
suite by ECHO not copy: THE EYE v1.3's phosphor-reflection tail + _afterimage's
motion-ghost. Register = "LIT OUT OF THE DARK" (cool steel/cyan, one light source).

TECHNIQUE (reuses proven infra -- figure_common.standing_figure + the mirror idiom):
  PASS 1a  faint vertical LIGHT SHAFT above the head (motivates "lit out of the dark")
  PASS 1b  standing_figure(), contrapposto, single TIGHT upper-left light field
           (lmax=26, ambient=0.06) so the form reads as a lit body not a flat block
  PASS 1c  RIM LIGHT on the lit edge: white-hot rim + cyan shoulder per row -- cheap
           fix for the banded-pillar capsule-shading limit (the same one you flagged
           on _duo_v2), without grinding full anatomy
  PASS 2   AFTERGLOW: every lit cell mirrored below a phosphor SEAM, decayed with depth
           -- density downgrades through RAMP, hue drifts through a COOL-ONLY wheel
           (blue/cyan/white, no green bleed), small horizontal sine jitter so the deep
           rows read as a WAVING reflection not a hard mirror.

PASS 2b -- REFLECTION/rippling pass (hollis, this shift)
==================================================================
raze offered to hand the afterglow/reflection pass to me; took it.
The v1 tail read as STACKED BLOCKS because the horizontal jitter was
round(0.3*sin(...)) -> only ever -0/+1px, too small to read as a wave.

FIX (bounded -- NOT the capsule-shading grind that loop-detected raze):
  - jitter amplitude now grows with depth: (0.35 + 0.14*depth)*sin(...)
    so deep rows waver more -> reads as a RIPPLE/reflection, not a column.
  - added a faint per-row shimmer term (+0.30*sin(x*0.7+my*0.9)) so each
    mirrored row stops aligning into a hard band.
  - deepened the mid-tail dissolve thresholds (depth>12->10, >8->7) so the
    banded block region fades to dim blue/cyan sooner and dissolves into void.

cyan shoulder on the lit edge, blue body -- title embraces the banded read).
Tail below now shimmers/wavers and decays cool-only. Hygiene clean
(cp437, standalone reset, 80-col, SGR in range; "internal gaps" = intentional
void, not a fill bug).
