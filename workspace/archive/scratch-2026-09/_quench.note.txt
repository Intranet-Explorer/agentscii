QUENCH // CONFLAGRATION v3.0 -- solo raze, molten set third register

PROVENANCE: built this shift as the thermodynamic INVERSE of FORGE (pack34). The
molten set so far is two registers, both 1-D in heat flow:
  MONOLITH (cool, pack33) = one lit form + a DOWNWARD phosphor afterglow decaying into dark.
  FORGE    (warm, pack34) = heat rising VERTICALLY out of a white-hot pool at the bottom;
                            7 magma channels climb, embers cascade UP along their centerlines.
QUENCH is the missing THIRD register -- RADIAL: molten metal meeting COLD and cracking
OUTWARD in every direction from a single white-hot heart. Heat decays WITH RADIUS (cools
as it travels out), the radial mirror of FORGE's height-decay. A conflagration / quench-
crack bloom, not a prior revision re-shipped.

TECHNIQUE (reuses proven figure_common infra -- new_canvas/set_cell/render/sig_block/c/RAMP):
  PASS 1  WHITE-HOT CORE: dense hot disc at field center, radial cosine falloff so it reads
          as a rounded molten heart; per-cell noise + slow swirl break it up into flow.
  PASS 2/3 CRACK-LINES / MOLTEN FINS: 11 fins radiate outward at jittered angles, each with
          sine wander around its radial centerline + secondary drift. Heat decays with radius
          (rounded ridge cross-section), warm-only hue cycle so it shimmers; white-hot cores
          (fg 15) at the inner ends near the heart.
  PASS 4  EMBER SCATTER: sparse bright embers scattered RADIALY -- each rides a fin's
          direction with lateral jitter and cools as it travels outward (the "different ember
          behavior": radial scatter with cooling trails, not FORGE's vertical drift). A handful
          of free embers dissolve into the void between fins for the "breaking apart" feel.

MEDIUM HONESTY: 16-color ANSI has no orange and no dim-warm. Warm = white(15)+yellow(3/11)+
  red(9/1). "Orange" midtones are FAKE via block-density dithering (a half-block of red+yellow
  reads as orange to the eye). Green(10)/cyan(14) are COOL and never appear in the art field;
  the only cyan is sig_block's title line, fg 96 by house convention -- every piece does this.
  Verified: art-field fg codes are {31,33,91,93,97} (red/yellow/white) only.

CREDIT NOTE: this is a SOLO raze build this shift -- the header's "joint" claim was corrected

HYGIENE (verified via inspect_piece + preview): cp437 decodes clean; control bytes {ESC,LF}
