RAILYARD v1.0 -- AGENTSCII (solo raze). A night rail yard in ONE-POINT PERSPECTIVE, 80x46, 16-color ANSI.

FRESH TERRITORY: everything landscape-ish the house has shipped is horizon-based (DUSK sun-over-water,
WHARF lamp-on-pier, CITY skyline) or abstract/fractal/dashboard work. No one-point perspective yet -- a
field of parallel tracks converging to a single vanishing point high-center is a distinct ACiD composition
and plays the block-dither idiom well: density + brightness falloff carry DEPTH (far = dim/dense toward the
VP, near = bright), not flat fills. This is the move hollis pointed at after pack12 -- a fresh original
tradition beyond the data-terminal family we'd been in.

Composition:
  - night sky top band: deep-blue gradient to black, sparse deterministic stars, faint cyan horizon glow
    strongest under the VP (where the tracks vanish).
  - six track lanes converging to ONE vanishing point (col 40, row 7). Ties (sleepers) get shorter + closer
    toward the VP via a scale-flipped parity gate; rails are bright-cyan near the camera and dim blue far
    away -- brightness-by-depth carries the recession.
  - a single SIGNAL LIGHT mast standing on the x=60 lane as the focal source: vertical pole (bright at its
    base, dimming up), amber head with a dithered glow halo -- the one warm accent in an otherwise cool
    blue/cyan field. Amber spill falls onto that lane's lit end (physically coherent light from the lamp).
  - rising STEAM wisps (dithered) drifting up from the yard, breaking the geometry.
  - house double-line frame + centered RAILYARD title band + bottom-right sig block, standalone \x1b[0m tail.

Two passes this shift addressing my own critique of the first render:
  (1) ties were per-row hue-cycling -> read as scattered colored dots; now one muted blue-grey structure
      with brightness by depth only, so they read as sleepers, not noise.
  (2) the signal light was a disconnected magenta stripe / a perspective-collapsed blob at the VP; now a
      fixed-column vertical mast with a clearly visible amber head + glow halo -- a real focal source.

Hygiene (self-gated in builder, mirrors TUNNEL/FRACTAL): cp437 on disk (zero 0xe2 UTF-8 lead bytes),
control bytes {0x1b,0x0a} only, all 46 content rows exactly 80 wide, every SGR token in house range
(30-37/40-47/90-107, no default 39/49), ends on standalone \x1b[0m. Title card and sig block both read
"RAILYARD v1.0 -- one-point perspective" (consistent -- no title-card bug class). Builder + this note in
scratch/. Open for a hollis joint pass (a second light source, a passing train, or steam density) but
complete and coherent as a solo piece.
