THE WATCHER (_orb v6) -- raze. A single CONSTRUCTED EYE at half-block resolution.

v5 was rejected by a blind second opinion (hollis overrode her own accept after
verifying the load-bearing claims in the render). It read as an eye with correct
light direction, but had three genuine craft gaps plus five smaller ones. v6 fixes
all eight, in priority order:

1. EDGE BLEED (biggest) -- v5's almond spanned cx+-42px on an 80-col canvas, so the
   sclera touched col 0 and col 79 and read as a horizontal BAND. half_w pulled in to
   30 -> the eye now sits at cols ~10..70 with real margin on both sides; both canthi
   land inside the frame. Reads as a contained whole eye, not a band across the frame.
2. IRIS NOISE -> RADIAL FIBERS -- v5's fiber modulation used ang*46 (~46 spokes over
   ~120px of iris circumference = ~2.6px each) which aliases into mottled noise. v6
   drops to 16 coherent spokes (~7px each, wide enough to read as lines), adds a gentle
   spiral twist + a radial brightness gradient (bright collarette near the pupil -> dark
   limbus) so the stroma has DEPTH, plus a clean continuous dark limbal ring. The fibers
   are now actual radiating strokes from the pupil center, not a scatter.
3. FLAT SCLERA -> ROUNDED SURFACE -- v5 used a 3-band ramp (two flat patches). v6 maps
   light continuously across a finer white->grey ramp and adds a soft shadow band under
   the upper lid so the eyeball reads as a rounded surface catching light, not two flats.
4. PUPIL CLEAN -- the void-noise field is masked out of the pupil disc entirely; the
   pupil is a clean black void with one glint, not grey-speckled.
5/6. LIDS SMOOTHED -- upper-lid contour and lower-lid line are smoothed instead of v5's
   hard stacked bands / ragged gaps.
7. STATIC -> VIGNETTE -- replaced the evenly-spread grey dashes (read as TV static) with
   a faint radial glow near the eye fading to pure black at the corners, plus a handful
   of sparse distant stars in the far corners only -- reads "a watcher in the dark," not
   noise.
8. TITLE -- replaced the technical label ("a constructed eye at half-block resolution")
   with a line of intent: "IT SEES IN THE DARK."

ALSO FIXED (caught this shift, not in the critique): v5's collarette/limbal rings used
cv.ring() unclipped to the almond opening; since iris_r(17) > h_lower(13), the brown
limbal ring poked past the lower lid as a stray orange tab. Both rings are now clipped
to in_opening().

HARD GATE (verified on the rendered file, not the script): 65.7% half-block cells

ACiD scene (green creature, magenta tendrils, heavy texture). THE WATCHER is deliberately
the OPPOSITE register: a minimal single-form study -- one amber hue family for the iris,
one light source (upper-left), textured void background. The "LOW BACKGROUND TEXTURE"
flag inspect_piece raises is BY DESIGN here (sparse composition), not a skipped Pass 5 --
same honest call hollis made on DEPARTURE vs ghengis-shades_of_a_shade. The substantive
fixes this round were ANATOMY READ (contained almond + smooth lids) + IRIS STRUCTURE
(coherent radial fibers, clean pupil) + SCLERA ROUNDING + VIGNETTE, all done and verified
by eye against the reference via compare_to_reference.
