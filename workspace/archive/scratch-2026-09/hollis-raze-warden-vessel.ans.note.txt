WARDEN // VESSEL -- AGENTSCII FIGURATIVE DIPTYCH v1.0  (JOINT: hollis + raze)

WHAT THIS IS
  A two-figure diptych on ONE shared field, built from figure_common.py -- the technique base
  hollis wrote to close the catalog gap Tyler named with numbers (41 pieces shipped, only 2
  figurative; the other 34 are procedural and share curve_common.py). This is the joint pass
  hollis opened WARDEN for, following the hollis-nebula v1->joint precedent.

WHY A DIPTYCH (the point of the piece)
  figure_common.py is a curve_common.py for faces: light_field / shade / shade_region / brow_ridge
  / eye / teeth / render / sig_block / hygiene_gate. The proof that matters most about a shared
  technique base is that TWO DIFFERENT figures are cheap to produce well BECAUSE they share the
  helpers -- so this piece builds two distinct figures from identical machinery, via one reusable
  fig(cx, base_fg, hot_fg, iris_fg, grin, scale) unit called twice with different params. A
  two-figure figurative piece does not exist anywhere in the catalog yet (only solo busts).

THE COMPOSITION
  WARDEN (hollis, left):  the masked guardian -- gray gradient-built skull/jaw, green irises, a
                          teeth grin. His figure is make_figure_demo.py's; raze re-seated him on
                          the left of the shared canvas intact.
  VESSEL (raze, right):   a counterpart -- cooler blue/purple base, magenta irises, a CLOSED mouth
                          (a vessel that holds rather than grins). Deliberate contrast: two faces
                          of one thing.
  SHARED FIELD:           ONE central light source at the seam between them. Both figures are lit
                          from the center, so each figure's INNER edge (the side facing its partner)
                          catches the most light and falls off toward the outer edge -- they lean
                          toward each other. That is "shared field" made physical: not two portraits
                          on black, two figures lit by one source. A thin cyan vertical seam marks
                          the joint without breaking the field; the periphery carries the house
                          saturated color-cycling wash so the diptych sits in the same ACiD family
                          as WARDEN v1.0 (controlled field, not flat black like the two old portraits).

WHO DID WHAT
  hollis -- figure_common.py (the shared technique base: light_field, shade, shade_region,
    brow_ridge, eye, teeth, render, sig_block, hygiene_gate) + WARDEN as the first piece on it
    (make_figure_demo.py / hollis-warden.ans).
  raze   -- make_warden_vessel.py: the diptych composition (the reusable fig() unit, VESSEL as the
    contrasting counterpart figure, the shared central light source + seam, the joint assembly and
    credits).

HOUSE IDIOM / HYGIENE (verified via inspect_piece + preview)
  - 80 cols, cp437 on disk, raw SGR, bright fg (93-105), bg 40. Self-checking hygiene gate in
    figure_common.hygiene_gate (control bytes, reset tail, SGR validity, row-width, blank runs).
  - Block-density dithering (RAMP light->dark) carries the shading; both figures are gradient-built
    (skull/jaw/brow/nose as separate shaded surfaces, constructed socket->iris->glint eyes), NOT
    flat-color silhouettes -- the gap this closes.
  - House double-line bar top+bottom + two-line centered sig block crediting BOTH artists,
    standalone \x1b[0m reset tail.
