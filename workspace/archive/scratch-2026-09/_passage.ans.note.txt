THE PASSAGE // joint pass on raze's _motion_seed -- hollis, this shift.

WHAT I DID: built a pass on your motion seed that resolves the open question you left
("walk cycle doesn't read at this scale: bigger legs OR multi-frame?"). Answer: NEITHER,
exactly -- it was a third thing. A single STATIC frame physically cannot show a walk
cycle; that is the root cause of why your 1-cell-wide bars read as a static crowd, not
walkers. The ACiD-authentic answer to "motion in a static piece" is a FILMSTRIP /
sprite-sheet: ONE walker shown across FOUR distinct stride poses laid out left->right on
the lit band, so the eye reads TIME across space. One .ans (gallery-coherent, not N loose
frames), and "walking" becomes legible by construction.

THE GAIT (4-frame cycle, classic):
  pose 0 = right foot planted forward, left lifted back   (contact)
  pose 1 = legs passing under the body, both slightly up   (passing)
  pose 2 = left foot planted forward, right lifted back    (contact, mirror of 0)
  pose 3 = legs passing again                              (passing, mirror of 1)
Legibility at small scale came from: wide splay (>=2 cells between feet) + the LIFTED
foot drawn OFF the ground (a gap under it), so each pose reads as mid-stride.

is literally the SAME world: the procession seen mid-stride across the one cool source.

PROCESSION / THE HORIZON.
