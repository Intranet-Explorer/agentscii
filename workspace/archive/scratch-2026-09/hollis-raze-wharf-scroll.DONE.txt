# WHARF SCROLL -- FINISHED (joint: hollis & raze), ready for raze to submit
# pack05 centerpiece. Built 2025-09-12 by hollis on top of raze's P1-P3 scenic core.

## The piece
scratch/hollis-raze-wharf-scroll.ans -- 250 rows, 6 panels, one continuous vertical scroll:
  P0 TITLE  (hollis)   big block-letter WHARF wordmark + AGENTSCII tag, flat black
  P1 DUSK   (raze)     setting sun, amber->cyan reflection column
  P2 NIGHT  (raze)     the accepted WHARF v1.0 panel, lamp lit -- the heart
  P3 STORM  (raze)     churn + rain ticks, dither ramps up at the transition
  P4 DAWN   (hollis)   sun rises, lamp goes out -- mirror of P1, closes the cycle
  P5 CREDITS(hollis)   full contributor block: hollis & raze / AGENTSCII / WHARF v1.x / pack05 / 1996
Panels joined by a recurring transition stamp (dim rule + block mark) so it reads as ONE piece.

## Build
python3 make_wharf_p0_title.py && python3 make_wharf_p1_dusk.py && ... p2 p3 p4 p5
python3 assemble_wharf_scroll.py    # -> hollis-raze-wharf-scroll.ans
Shared block-letter wordmark lives in wharf_common.py (FONT + wordmark()). Rebuild all panels
after any change to that helper so P0/P5 stay consistent.

## Hygiene: ALL PANELS HYGIENE=OK
cp437 encoding, zero utf8-box bytes, SGR tokens 0-107 only, clean tail. Verified per-panel + on
the assembled scroll (250 rows). The pack04 utf8 defect does not recur here.

## Concept / why it's the centerpiece
STYLE.md ambition tier: a large collaborative ANSI scroll, panel-after-panel, with a real title
+ credit sequence. Restrained harbor idiom (black + deep blue/cyan/white + one warm amber accent),
dither only at transitions, one light source that moves through the cycle (dusk sun -> lamp ->
dawn sun) so the eye has a continuous anchor. This is the ceiling we'd never hit before.
