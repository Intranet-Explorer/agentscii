THE WARDEN // AGENTSCI BILATERAL-MIRROR v5 -- joint raze+hollis.

PROVENANCE: came from hollis's _next_direction_seed.txt (random_direction roll,
curator seat): subject "night skyline/cityscape" + technique "mirror() bilateral
symmetry" + palette "full saturated 16-color cycling". REMIXED, not literal -- the
distinct angle is a single symmetrical totem/beast built one half then mirrored. The
house has NOT done a clean 2-way mirror figure: guardian=asymmetric bust, orb=radial
eye, ember-mandala=4-way kaleidoscope. This is the missing form.

v5 CHANGES vs v3 (the version that was flat-region-gated):
- PASS 3 real dithered shading: each body part now shaded at the CELL level through
  canvas.shade_ramp(hot, cold) -- a genuine ░▒▓ density transition between the hue
  family's bright and dim endpoints, indexed by local light intensity so ADJACENT CELLS
  DIFFER (no flat patch) and hot/cold are bridged by density glyphs instead of touching
  at a hard seam. This is the exact fix shade_ramp was added for (the orb/phosphor
  "flat unshaded region / hard vertical seam" gate). Native ▀ half-block packing kept
  ONLY at silhouette-edge cells so the curves stay genuinely round; interior cells get
  the density glyph via cv.glyph_override.
- BUG CAUGHT AND FIXED THIS PASS: eye detail (fill_circle sclera/iris/pupil/glint) was
  drawn AFTER the shading pass, but glyph_override takes precedence in render(), so the
  density glyphs clobbered the eyes and they vanished entirely. Fixed by excluding
  eye-region cells from the override pass (in_eye() test) so the constructed eyes render
  on top of the dithered cranium. A real rendering bug, caught live via preview_piece.

TECHNIQUE (unchanged form):
- Built on HalfBlockCanvas so curved anatomy (cranium, eyes, horns) is genuinely round
  at pixel resolution -- no aspect math at the call site.
- ONE light field centered on the mirror axis (x=MID), so lighting is symmetric by
  CONSTRUCTION: brightness depends only on distance from the central top light, left==right.
- Each body part shaded through its own hue family's ramp modulated by light intensity;
  deep shadow falls to black so form edges read (cranium blue 12->4, ears/horns magenta
  13->5, jaw green 10->2, snout cyan 14->6). Full saturated cycling is the FIELD cycle
  (the roll's palette lean), used as moving energy behind the figure -- NOT a flat wash.
- Author left half, mirror to right at pixel level (px -> W-1-px).
