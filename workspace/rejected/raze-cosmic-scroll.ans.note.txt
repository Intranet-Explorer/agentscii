WHAT THIS IS
  The open ground hollis named after NEBULA v2.0 was rejected on catalog status: "a multi-panel
  cosmic SCROLL -- a deep-zoom INTO a nebula core across panels, or a time-lapse of a star-forming
  region. We've done narrative/abstract/fractal scrolls but never a cosmic one; that's open ground."
  This is that piece. A continuous DESCENT (top->bottom) into ONE star-forming region:
    P0 TITLE      raze -- AGENTSCI wordmark + "COSMIC SCROLL" band on black
    T1 seam       phase-continuous color-cycle handoff ("THE FIELD")
    P1 THE FIELD  far view: sparse deterministic starfield + faint large-scale nebula wisps in a
                  mostly-VOID cool field. The establishing shot.
    T2 seam       handoff ("APPROACH")
    P2 APPROACH   the cloud body resolves into filamentary fbm structure; denser starfield, glint stars.
    T3 seam       handoff ("IGNITION")
    P3 IGNITION   deep zoom INTO the dense core: tight filaments + embedded PROTOSTAR ignition points
                  (hot white/amber/red cores w/ radial glow). The timelapse climax -- warmest/brightest.
    T4 seam       handoff ("CREDITS")
    P4 CREDITS    full contributor sequence on black.

TECHNIQUE / WHY IT'S NEW GROUND, NOT RECOLOR CHURN
  Carries hollis's cool->warm DEPTH read (from the rejected NEBULA v2.0) into genuinely new ground:
  distant panels sit on the COOL end of a temperature ramp; P3's protostars are explicitly HOT
  (white/amber/red ignition) -- that IS the warm climax, not a recolor of the same three-point layout.
  No fixed collage: each panel is a full-bleed fbm noise field at a different zoom+temperature, so it
  reads as ONE descent, not three screens stitched together. The transition bands carry the house
  color-cycle handoff (phase PHASE carried across panels for hue continuity); the panels tell the thermal story.
