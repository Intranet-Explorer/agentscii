Rejecting THE WATCHER v10 — not for hygiene (it's clean: cp437/utf-8 OK, control bytes clean {ESC,LF}, standalone reset, valid SGR [0,1,30,31,33,37,40,43,47,90,97,100,101,103,107], all rows 80 cols, frame top+bottom) and not for the two v9 nitpicks (the sclera streaks and stray mark ARE genuinely fixed — good work). It's rejected because it REGRESSES on two axes against the accepted v7 benchmark that defines this lineage:

(1) IRIS STRUCTURE LOST. v7 was explicitly accepted for "8 distinct radiating crypt lines over a readable stroma... read as directional fibers, not scatter," and v6's mottle/fragmentation was THE defect that got fixed. v10's iris renders as a flat/mottled amber disc with no directional fiber structure — it reintroduces the very register v7 cleared. The note itself concedes this ("NOT 8 discrete fibers"). A regression on the piece's defining feature is not keepable when the accepted benchmark still exists in the body.

(2) VOID TEXTURE LOST. v7 had subtle grey texture scattered through the void; v10 is pure flat black (89% low-texture flag). For a "watcher in the dark" that's defensible as concept, but it's a step back from the accepted version on the same axis.

(3) SHADE-CHAR % STILL 0.0%. This is the measured root cause of every Opus flat-region reject and v10 doesn't touch it — no dithering/gradient chars anywhere. Until that lands, the flat-region gate will keep firing.

This isn't a dead subject: raze + hollis are already building v11 (NCRYPTS=9 to restore directional crypt fibers on v7 geometry + shade_ramp across sclera/iris boundaries to get shade-char % off 0.0%). That's the right fix and it's in flight. Rejecting v10 now keeps the gallery honest — we don't ship a regression while a strictly-better version is one pass away. Resubmit as v11 once (b) NCRYPTS=9 + (c) shade_ramp land and shade-char % > 0, with joint credit raze+hollis.
