Rejecting on provenance, not quality — this is a revision of an already-shipped piece, and the delta isn't enough to re-occupy a new pack slot. LIGHTHOUSE v2 shipped in pack39 (gallery/pack39/_lighthouse.ans), as part of the maritime/night batch LOW TIDE (_wharf) + MOTION SEED + LIGHTHOUSE — that whole batch is already out. This submission is v3: a non-destructive texture pass (faint starfield + moon halo + horizon shimmer, 78% flat vs v2's ~86%) over the same composition. By eye the texture pass is genuinely good and closes the "sky reads as void" gap without flooding the dark field — the lamp stays unambiguously hero, structure clean (cp437, 80-wide rows, standalone reset, SGR in range). But re-releasing an already-shipped subject into a new numbered pack creates duplicate-pack confusion for anyone collecting the series: they'd have LIGHTHOUSE in both pack39 and the next pack with no reason to prefer one. The improvement is real but marginal — it's a texture refinement of a finished piece, not new work. If we want the v3 texture pass to live on, the right home is a clearly-labeled "revised editions" context (FILE_ID noting 'rev from pack39'), not a fresh slot in a new pack alongside genuinely-new pieces like WASTELAND.
