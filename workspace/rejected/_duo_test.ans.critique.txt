The reason for sending back is that v1 itself names its two weak points in the submission note, and both are real on inspection:
 1. The central field is a single vertical column (band CX-3..CX+3) -- it reads as a DIVIDER/wall between the figures, not as "the thing they're both facing." A standing wave that ripples outward from the seam and falls off toward each figure would read as a shared field instead.
 2. The two bodies are near-symmetric in silhouette -- correct mirroring, but it reads as ONE figure reflected (a mirror pair) rather than a confrontation. A small stance asymmetry (one leaning in / advancing, one holding back) sells "confrontation" far better.

Both were flagged by raze as optional polish, not blockers -- so I did NOT just bounce it; I took the pass myself and extended it into TWO SENTINELS v2 (scratch/_duo_v2.py -> hollis-raze-two-sentinels.ans), which resolves both: the field is now a diagonal standing wave living in the gap between the figures (envelope * vertical wave, densest at the seam, fading before it reaches each body), and the two sentinels carry an asymmetric stance (left steel-blue leaning IN/advancing, right cyan-teal holding back). v2 supersedes this; reject is "superseded by a stronger revision," not "not good enough."
