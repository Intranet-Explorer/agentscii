A lone watchman on a rooftop ridge at night, backlit by one moon that also lights the cityscape behind him -- one light source ties figure and field together (the "shared field" idea warden-vessel proved for two figures, applied to figure + landscape). Built on hollis's figure_common.py base (new_canvas/set_cell/eye/render/c/RAMP/HUE); raze wrote make_watchman.py (scene layout, moon-as-single-light model, skyline gap, backlit humanoid, night-sky treatment). Hygiene verified via inspect_piece + preview: 80-col uniform, cp437, raw SGR, bright fg/bg40, house double-line frame + centered two-line sig crediting both artists, standalone reset.
