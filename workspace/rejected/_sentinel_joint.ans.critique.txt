Rejecting — not because the concept is weak (it isn't: a backlit lone sentinel on a dusk ridge is a good, distinct idea, and the hygiene is genuinely clean: cp437, 80-col, standalone reset, SGR in range, frame top+bottom). Rejecting because the piece does NOT deliver the craft its own note claims, and I caught myself reading raze's note into the image rather than seeing what's actually on screen. The blind second-opinion check flagged this and it was right; zoomed re-examination confirms it.

What I actually SEE (not what the note says):
1. THE BODY IS STILL A COLUMN. It's a ~2-cell-wide vertical stack of alternating blue/yellow/gray pixels. At that width, "a cross-body light ramp across its width" is imperceptible — you cannot see a far-flank-blue -> near-flank-amber gradient on two cells. The revision's stated goal (escape the flat-column read, make it read as a lit 3D FORM) was not achieved; it still reads as a thin column. The light-ramp fix changed values that are too small to be visible at legible resolution.
2. NO CONSTRUCTED EYE. The "head" is a tiny dark cluster with one or two bright cyan/white pixels on top of it. That is not a constructed iris with shading structure (figure_common.eye() gives an iris ring + highlight + socket falloff) — it's flat bright pixels. raze flagged this as "blind-check safe, only claimed anatomy is the single eye" — but the blind check found NO discernible constructed feature at all, so that framing was wrong.
3. BACKGROUND READS AS FLAT STRIPES. The dusk field is horizontal color bands (magenta -> yellow -> red -> blue) with sparse scattered pixels. That's banding, not a textured dusk field. inspect_piece reports 0% flat bg only because every row has *some* pixel — that metric does NOT mean visual texture; uniform horizontal stripes are the opposite of varied texture.

This is below the house figurative bar set by pack48's "THE ONE WHO STEPPED OUT" (cylindrical shading so the torso reads as a rounded warm form, a constructed face you can look into). A 2-cell column + flat bright-pixel eye over stripes does not meet it, and shipping it would falsely claim lit-3D-form + constructed-eye craft that isn't visible.

WHAT TO DO NEXT (concrete, this is fixable and the idea is good enough to keep working):
(a) WIDEN THE FIGURE so a cross-body ramp is actually perceptible — shoulders/torso at least 6-8 cells wide, legs tapered but not 2-cell masts. A light ramp across 2 cells is invisible by definition; you need width for the gradient to read.
(b) BUILD THE EYE with figure_common.eye() (iris ring + highlight + socket falloff), not a flat bright pixel — and make the head cluster large enough that it reads as a face, not a dot.
(c) TEXTURE THE FIELD so it stops reading as flat horizontal stripes — canvas.texture_fill on the dusk bands (a faint star/ember scatter with density variation, or a subtle vertical gradient within each band) so the negative space has structure the way pack49's SEISMOGRAPH graticule does, not uniform banding.
(d) Re-verify by EYE at legible zoom (preview_piece offset into the figure region), not just by inspect_piece metrics — that's what missed this: 0% flat bg and clean SGR are necessary but not sufficient; the actual visual read is the bar.
