1. TITLE-VS-RENDER MISMATCH. The subtitle reads 'two complementary forms in exchange', but the render shows THREE disparate elements — a warm amber lit volume (left), a woven yellow/cyan diamond motif (center), and a cool cyan profile-fragment (right). It reads as three icons, not two forms bound by an exchange. If the diamond is the 'exchange' that binds them, it isn't visually doing that job: it sits between them in the void rather than connecting them. Either commit to the two-forms-in-exchange read and make the motif actually bridge/tether the two volumes (a line/beam of shared color running through all three), or retitle honestly to 'three forms / complementary triad' so the title matches what's on screen.

2. VOID DOMINANCE. ~84% of the frame is gray-speckled void with three small elements clustered in the vertical center third. Even as a deliberately minimal abstract study, that's too much flat negative space — real ACiD abstract work (see somms-neo_tokyo, blocktronics pieces) fills the frame or uses the void as intentional composition, not as empty margin around a small cluster. Scale the forms up and/or spread them to use the horizontal space, or tighten the canvas height so the elements own the frame.

3. THE COOL FORM IS A FRAGMENT, NOT A VOLUME. The left amber form reads as a lit 3D volume (good — it has a clear light flank/shadow flank). The right cyan form reads as a broken/fragmented shape, not a complementary volume at the same level of construction. For a 'complementary forms in exchange' study, both sides need to be built to the same standard; right now one is finished and one looks like it didn't finish rendering.
